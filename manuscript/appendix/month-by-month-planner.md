# Appendix: Month-by-Month Senior Year Planner

*Status: outline only — draft not started.*

## Covers

- **August–September** — Finalize your college list. Start the Common App. Request transcripts. Begin brainstorming your essay.
- **October** — Take or retake the SAT/ACT. Draft essays. Request letters of recommendation. Submit Early Action/Early Decision applications.
- **November** — Finish supplemental essays. Submit regular-decision applications with early deadlines. FAFSA opens — file it.
- **December** — Continue regular-decision applications. Confirm recommenders have submitted. Watch for mid-year grade requests.
- **January** — Submit remaining regular-decision applications. Meet CSS Profile deadlines. Apply for outside scholarships.
- **February** — Finish scholarship applications. Respond promptly to any financial aid follow-up requests.
- **March** — Decisions start arriving. Begin comparing offers as they come in.
- **April** — Compare final financial aid packages. Visit or revisit top schools. Decide before May 1.
- **May** — Send your deposit. Decline other offers. Take AP exams. Send your final transcript to your chosen school.
- **June–August** — Complete housing forms, orientation, and health requirements. Connect with your roommate. Prepare for move-in.
