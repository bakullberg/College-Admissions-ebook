    References: Finding Your Fit¶

    1. Public university - Wikipedia - Explains how state-funded universities differ from private colleges in mission, cost and in-state preference, background for the chapter's discussion of financial fit and residency.

    2. Research university - Wikipedia - Describes large universities focused on research and graduate study, helping students weigh school size, class size and academic fit against smaller colleges.

    3. Cost of attendance - Wikipedia - Defines the full yearly cost colleges publish, including tuition, room and board and indirect costs, the starting point for the chapter's sticker price and financial fit sections.

    4. Colleges That Change Lives (revised ed.) - Loren Pope, revised by Hilary Masell Oswald - Penguin Books - Pope, a former New York Times education editor, argued that fit and teaching matter more than prestige. His profiles of 40 small colleges launched a movement that echoes this chapter's "no single right school" message.

    5. Where You Go Is Not Who You'll Be: An Antidote to the College Admissions Mania - Frank Bruni - Grand Central Publishing (2015) - Bruni profiles successful people who attended less selective colleges, making the case that what students do in college matters more than its name. A readable antidote to choosing by rankings.

    6. Sizing Up Colleges: Big vs. Small - College Board BigFuture - Weighs the trade-offs of large and small colleges, from class size to course variety, matching the chapter's school size section.

    7. Campus Setting: Rural, Suburban, or Urban - College Board BigFuture - Describes how campus setting shapes daily life, transportation and internships, supporting the chapter's campus setting and distance from home sections.

    8. 3 Steps to Creating a College List - College Board BigFuture - Short guide to building a balanced list of reach, target and safety schools, a companion to the chapter's Balanced List Builder MicroSim.

    9. Focus on Net Price, Not Sticker Price - College Board BigFuture - Explains why the published price rarely matches what families pay and how to estimate net price, reinforcing the chapter's financial fit section.

    10. The True Cost of Attendance - College Board BigFuture - Breaks the cost of attendance into tuition, housing, food and indirect costs, helping students build the yearly budget this chapter introduces.
