    References: The College Landscape and Application Rounds¶

    1. College admissions in the United States - Wikipedia - Overview of how American colleges admit students, including selectivity, application rounds, holistic review and the role of admissions offices. A broad background for every term this chapter introduces.

    2. Early decision - Wikipedia - Explains binding Early Decision, its history, Early Decision II and the debate over whether it favors wealthier applicants. Useful context for the chapter's comparison of application rounds.

    3. Liberal arts college - Wikipedia - Describes the small, undergraduate-focused colleges the chapter contrasts with research and flagship universities, including their teaching emphasis, class sizes and breadth of required study.

    4. The Gatekeepers: Inside the Admissions Process of a Premier College - Jacques Steinberg - Viking (2002) - Steinberg, a New York Times reporter, spent a year inside Wesleyan University's admissions office, following one officer and several applicants. His narrative shows how readers actually work through files, rounds and committee decisions.

    5. The Truth about College Admission: A Family Guide to Getting In and Staying Together - Brennan Barnard and Rick Clark - Johns Hopkins University Press (2019) - Written by a high school counselor and Georgia Tech's director of undergraduate admission, it explains each step from both sides of the desk, stressing that decisions reflect a college's institutional priorities, not a verdict on the student.

    6. Facts About Early Decision and Early Action - College Board BigFuture - Plain-language comparison of Early Decision, Early Action and Restrictive Early Action, with the pros and cons of applying early. A good companion to the chapter's rounds table.

    7. Early Decision and Early Action Calendar - College Board BigFuture - Month-by-month calendar of early application deadlines and decision dates, helping students see how early rounds fit into the fall of senior year.

    8. How Do College Acceptance Rates Work? - College Board BigFuture - Explains what admission rates measure, why they vary by round and why they don't predict any one student's chances, reinforcing the chapter's section on selectivity.

    9. Types of Colleges: The Basics - College Board BigFuture - Short guide to public, private, liberal arts, research and other kinds of colleges, matching the four-year college categories laid out in this chapter.

    10. National Association for College Admission Counseling - NACAC - The professional association of counselors and admissions officers. Publishes ethical standards for application rounds and deposits, plus student guides to the admission process.
