## About This MicroSim

Help students organize every task between committing and move-in day on a timeline with deadlines, showing what's due soon and flagging summer-melt risks.

**Learning objective:** Students will organize their post-commitment tasks on a timeline from May to move-in day and carry them out before their deadlines, recognizing which missed steps put enrollment at risk.

This MicroSim supports [Chapter 20: Getting Ready for Fall](../../chapters/20-getting-ready-for-fall/index.md).

## How to Use

- Enter your college and move-in date. Each task has an editable date and a checkbox.
- On wide screens, the timeline shows every task in its lane: green done, yellow due within 14 days, red overdue, blue coming up. Hover over a dot for details. Narrow screens list tasks by date.
- The **Melt risk** meter rises with overdue Money and Records tasks. Change **Today** to a summer date to practice.
- Use **Add task** for your college's extra steps and **Load Iowa example** for the chapter's dates. Fill in **Who to call** with your college's contacts. Everything is saved in this browser.

## Lesson Plan

### Learning Objective

Students will organize their post-commitment tasks on a timeline from May to move-in day and carry them out before their deadlines, recognizing which missed steps put enrollment at risk.

**Bloom level:** Apply (L3) — organize, carry out

### Audience

High school juniors and seniors, and the parents, counselors, and mentors helping them.

### Duration

10 minutes now, then weekly through the summer

### Prerequisites

Read the section of [Chapter 20](../../chapters/20-getting-ready-for-fall/index.md) that introduces this MicroSim.

### Activities

1. **Iowa example (3 min):** Load the Iowa example and set Today to July 5. Which overdue tasks raise the melt risk?
2. **Your summer (5 min):** Enter your college's real dates from its admitted-student portal.
3. **Who to call (2 min):** Find and enter your college's first-year, financial aid, and housing contacts.

### Discussion Questions

- What is summer melt, and which tasks most often cause it?
- Why do housing and orientation dates matter so much in May?
- Who would you call first if a bill or form stopped you?

### Assessment

Students enter every task for their college with real dates and identify the three tasks that most threaten enrollment if missed.
