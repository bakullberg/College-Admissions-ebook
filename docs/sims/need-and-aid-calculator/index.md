## About This MicroSim

Show how cost of attendance, Student Aid Index, and a college's aid package combine to produce demonstrated need, net price, and unmet need, and how "meets full need" policies change the outcome.

**Learning objective:** Students will calculate demonstrated need, net price, and unmet need from a cost of attendance, Student Aid Index, and aid package, and compare outcomes at colleges with different aid policies.

This MicroSim supports [Chapter 14: Understanding Financial Aid](../../chapters/14-understanding-financial-aid/index.md).

## How to Use

- Set the **Cost of attendance** and **Student Aid Index**. The top bar splits the cost into the SAI (gray) and demonstrated need (blue).
- Set **Grants**, **Work-study**, and **Loans**. The second bar shows how the college fills the need. Red stripes mark unmet need.
- Check **This college meets full need** to fill any gap with grant aid, or **Allow aid above need (merit)** to let merit aid exceed need.
- Load the three presets to compare Marcus's colleges and a gapping college. Hover over any segment for its definition.

## Lesson Plan

### Learning Objective

Students will calculate demonstrated need, net price, and unmet need from a cost of attendance, Student Aid Index, and aid package, and compare outcomes at colleges with different aid policies.

**Bloom level:** Apply (L3) — calculate, compare

### Audience

High school juniors and seniors, and the parents, counselors, and mentors helping them.

### Duration

10–15 minutes

### Prerequisites

Read the section of [Chapter 14](../../chapters/14-understanding-financial-aid/index.md) that introduces this MicroSim.

### Activities

1. **Same SAI, different need (4 min):** Load both Marcus presets. Why is his need five times larger at the private college?
2. **The gap (4 min):** Load Gapping college. How much would the family need to find, and what does checking Meets full need change?
3. **Loans aren't discounts (4 min):** Raise the loan slider. Does the net price change? Why not?

### Discussion Questions

- Why isn't the Student Aid Index the amount your family will pay?
- Why can a college with a higher sticker price end up cheaper?
- What does a "meets full need" policy promise, and what doesn't it promise?

### Assessment

Students calculate demonstrated need, net price, and unmet need for a new set of numbers and match the calculator.
