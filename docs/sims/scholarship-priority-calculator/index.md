## About This MicroSim

Help students prioritize scholarship applications by estimating expected value per hour, adjusting for renewability and essay reuse, and seeing how a limited number of hours is best spent.

**Learning objective:** Students will prioritize a list of scholarships by calculating expected value per hour of work and judge how renewability and reusable essays change the ranking.

This MicroSim supports [Chapter 15: Scholarships](../../chapters/15-scholarships/index.md).

## How to Use

- Add each scholarship with its amount, number of awards, estimated applicants, and hours to apply. Check **Renewable** (and enter the years) and **Can reuse an existing essay** where they apply.
- Cards are ranked by expected value per hour, with the formula written out. Green cards fit your time budget.
- Move **Hours I have for scholarships this season** to see which applications fit and the expected total dollars.
- Hover over a card to see how renewal or essay reuse changed its rank. Load **Marcus's three** or **Ana's top eight** to compare the presets.

## Lesson Plan

### Learning Objective

Students will prioritize a list of scholarships by calculating expected value per hour of work and judge how renewability and reusable essays change the ranking.

**Bloom level:** Evaluate (L5) — prioritize, judge

### Audience

High school juniors and seniors, and the parents, counselors, and mentors helping them.

### Duration

15 minutes

### Prerequisites

Read the section of [Chapter 15](../../chapters/15-scholarships/index.md) that introduces this MicroSim.

### Activities

1. **Check Marcus (3 min):** Confirm $125, $50, and $6 per hour by hand. Why does the smallest award rank first?
2. **Renewal matters (4 min):** In Ana's list, uncheck Renewable on the first-generation nonprofit (by editing a copy). How far does it drop?
3. **Your season (8 min):** Enter your own scholarships and a realistic hour budget.

### Discussion Questions

- Why can a likely $1,000 be worth more than an unlikely $10,000?
- How does reusing an essay change which scholarships are worth applying for?
- Where can you find out how many students applied last year?

### Assessment

Students rank five scholarships by value per hour and justify which to apply for within a fixed time budget.
