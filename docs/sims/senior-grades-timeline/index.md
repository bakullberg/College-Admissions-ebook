## About This MicroSim

Show students the points in senior year when colleges receive information about their senior courses and grades, and how the application round changes what colleges see before deciding.

**Learning objective:** Students will explain when colleges receive senior-year course and grade information and interpret how that timing affects early, regular, and deferred applicants.

This MicroSim supports [Chapter 7: Your Senior Year Course Load](../../chapters/07-senior-year-course-load/index.md).

## How to Use

- Click any event to read what colleges learn at that point.
- Choose an applicant type in **Show me as** to highlight the events that matter for that applicant in gold and dim the rest.
- The shaded bands show which semester counts for the mid-year report and which for the final transcript.
- Drag sideways or use **◀**, **▶**, and **Show all** to move along the timeline.

## Lesson Plan

### Learning Objective

Students will explain when colleges receive senior-year course and grade information and interpret how that timing affects early, regular, and deferred applicants.

**Bloom level:** Understand (L2) — explain, interpret

### Audience

High school juniors and seniors, and the parents, counselors, and mentors helping them.

### Duration

10 minutes

### Prerequisites

Read the section of [Chapter 7](../../chapters/07-senior-year-course-load/index.md) that introduces this MicroSim.

### Activities

1. **Compare views (4 min):** Switch between Early Action and Regular Decision. Which grades does each college see before deciding?
2. **The deferral (3 min):** Choose Deferred early applicant. What is the strongest thing a deferred student can add to their file?
3. **Spring semester (3 min):** Read the final transcript and grade-drop events. Why does spring of senior year still matter after you're admitted?

### Discussion Questions

- Why can colleges judge your senior-year rigor before any senior grades exist?
- Why does the mid-year report matter more for Regular Decision than for Early Action applicants?
- What happens if your final transcript shows a serious drop?

### Assessment

Students explain which senior-year grade information a given applicant's college sees before the decision, and what the final transcript confirms.
