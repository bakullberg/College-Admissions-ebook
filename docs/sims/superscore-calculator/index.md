## About This MicroSim

Show how superscoring combines the best section scores across test dates, and compare the superscore with each single-sitting total.

**Learning objective:** Students will calculate an SAT or ACT superscore from multiple test dates and compare it with each single-sitting composite.

This MicroSim supports [Chapter 6: The SAT and ACT Question](../../chapters/06-sat-act-question/index.md).

## How to Use

- Choose **SAT** or **ACT**, then enter each section score for each test date. Each row shows that date's own total or composite.
- The bars compare each section across dates. The best score in each section is outlined in gold.
- The second chart and the readout compare the superscore (green) with the best single sitting (blue).
- Use **Add test date** (up to four) and **Remove last**, or load the chapter's **Priya** (SAT) and **Casey** (ACT) examples.

## Lesson Plan

### Learning Objective

Students will calculate an SAT or ACT superscore from multiple test dates and compare it with each single-sitting composite.

**Bloom level:** Apply (L3) — calculate, compare

### Audience

High school juniors and seniors, and the parents, counselors, and mentors helping them.

### Duration

10 minutes

### Prerequisites

Read the section of [Chapter 6](../../chapters/06-sat-act-question/index.md) that introduces this MicroSim.

### Activities

1. **Check the examples (3 min):** Confirm Priya's superscore of 1330 and Casey's 25 by hand.
2. **Why no gain? (3 min):** Casey's superscore equals the best single sitting. Explain why, using the rounding rule.
3. **Plan a retest (4 min):** Add a third date for Priya with a higher Math score. How much does the superscore move?

### Discussion Questions

- Why can a superscore be higher than any single test date?
- Why does rounding sometimes erase a superscore gain on the ACT?
- Why should you check each college's superscoring policy before you retest?

### Assessment

Students calculate a superscore from two or three test dates and compare it with the best single sitting.
