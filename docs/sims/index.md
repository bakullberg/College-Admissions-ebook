# MicroSims

MicroSims are small interactive simulations, each focused on one idea from the book. Every chapter embeds its sims, and each one also has its own lesson page with a fullscreen link and teaching notes.

Every sim below is marked **built** (orange dot): implemented and awaiting the author's review.


## Chapter 1: The College Landscape and Application Rounds

- [College Admissions Process Roadmap](admissions-process-roadmap/index.md): An interactive road map of the six stages of the college admissions process, with a drag-and-drop quiz on their order.
- [Senior Year Admissions Timeline](senior-year-admissions-timeline/index.md): An interactive timeline of one senior year in admissions, with a personal track that changes as you choose Regular Decision, Early Action, or Early Decision.
- [College Type Sorter](college-type-sorter/index.md): A drag-and-drop sorting activity: classify twelve real colleges as public or private and as a research university or liberal arts college, then decide which public research universities are flagships.
- [Admission Rate Calculator](admission-rate-calculator/index.md): Calculate a college's admission rate from its applications and admits, and see why a surge in applications lowers the rate even when admits stay the same.
- [The Journey of an Application File](application-file-journey/index.md): A swimlane flowchart that walks an application through an admissions office, showing who touches the file at each of seven steps.
- [Application Round Explorer](application-round-explorer/index.md): Answer five questions about one college and one student, and the explorer recommends an application round, explains why, and shows its deadline, decision, and commitment dates.

## Chapter 2: How Colleges Evaluate Applicants

- [Annotated Transcript Explorer](annotated-transcript-explorer/index.md): Explore a fictional high school transcript region by region and learn what each part tells an admissions reader.
- [GPA Calculator](gpa-calculator/index.md): Calculate an unweighted GPA from your own courses and see how credits, single grades, and a college's core-only recalculation change the result.
- [Rigor in Context](rigor-in-context/index.md): See why course rigor is judged against what your high school offers: compare the share of available advanced courses taken, not the raw count.
- [Holistic Review Rating Explorer](holistic-review-explorer/index.md): Compare applicant files with similar grades and see how a first reader's separate Academic, Activities, and Personal ratings respond to each part of the file, and to context.
- [Regent Admission Index Calculator](rai-calculator/index.md): Run the University of Iowa's Regent Admission Index formula and see how GPA, ACT score, core courses, and residency decide whether you clear the admission threshold.
- [Middle 50% Range Plotter](middle-50-range-plotter/index.md): Plot your GPA and ACT score against a college's middle 50% range and see whether you fall below, inside, or above it.

## Chapter 3: Finding Your Fit

- [College Self-Assessment Builder](college-self-assessment/index.md): A five-step self-assessment of majors, size, setting, culture, and cost that turns your answers into a ranked priority profile with dealbreakers.
- [Campus Size and Setting Explorer](campus-size-setting-explorer/index.md): Compare typical campus experiences across twelve combinations of school size and setting, highlight your target zone, and pin the colleges on your list.
- [Cost of Attendance Builder](cost-of-attendance-builder/index.md): Build a college's cost of attendance from tuition, room and board, and indirect costs, and compare how residency and housing choices change the total.
- [Reach, Target, Safety Classifier](reach-target-safety-classifier/index.md): Classify a college as a reach, target, or safety for you from your GPA and test score, its admission rate and middle 50% ranges, and its financial fit.
- [Balanced College List Builder](balanced-list-builder/index.md): Build a college list of eight to twelve schools across reach, target, and safety, and check live whether it is balanced and includes an affordable safety you'd be glad to attend.

## Chapter 4: Researching and Narrowing Your College List

- [Research Tool Matrix](research-tool-matrix/index.md): A matrix showing which college research tools best answer which questions, from admission chances and cost to campus culture.
- [College Outcomes Comparison](college-outcomes-comparison/index.md): Compare retention, four-year, and six-year graduation rates for up to four colleges, and see which gaps are a reason to ask questions.
- [List Narrowing Funnel](list-narrowing-funnel/index.md): Run Marcus's 18-college long list through three narrowing passes (fit, balance, and picturing yourself there) and watch each cut get logged with its reason.

## Chapter 5: Getting Organized

- [College Requirements Matrix](college-requirements-matrix/index.md): Compare application requirements across every college on your list, spot the one-of-a-kind requirements before they're missed, and total the workload.
- [Getting Organized Setup Checklist](organized-setup-checklist/index.md): An interactive checklist of the five first steps and the core setup tasks for getting organized, with a progress ring and a note on why each task matters.

## Chapter 6: The SAT and ACT Question

- [SAT-ACT Score Converter](sat-act-converter/index.md): Convert between SAT totals and ACT composites with the official concordance, and place the converted score in a college's middle 50% range.
- [Score Submission Planner](score-submission-planner/index.md): Compare your SAT or ACT score with every college on your list and get a college-by-college recommendation on whether to submit it, with the reason shown.
- [Superscore Calculator](superscore-calculator/index.md): Calculate an SAT or ACT superscore from several test dates and compare it with your best single-sitting score.

## Chapter 7: Your Senior Year Course Load

- [Advanced Course Pathways Compared](advanced-pathways-compared/index.md): Compare Advanced Placement, the International Baccalaureate, and dual enrollment on sign-up, commitment, assessment, college credit, and fit, and find the pathway that matches your situation.
- [When Colleges See Senior Grades](senior-grades-timeline/index.md): A timeline of when colleges receive senior-year course and grade information, and how that timing differs for early, regular, and deferred applicants.
- [Senior Schedule Balance Planner](senior-schedule-balance/index.md): Build a draft senior schedule and weekly commitments, then compare the rigor signal it sends with the weekly hours it demands, to find a sustainable plan.

## Chapter 8: Activities and Resume

- [Depth vs. Breadth Activity Map](depth-vs-breadth-map/index.md): Plot activities by years of involvement and weekly hours to see the difference between a deep activity profile and a broad one, then plot your own.
- [Activity Entry Builder](activity-entry-builder/index.md): Draft a Common App-style activity entry within the real character limits and get instant feedback on action verbs, specifics, and hours.
- [What Counts as an Activity?](what-counts-activity-sorter/index.md): Decide whether fourteen everyday commitments, from jobs and family duties to hobbies, belong on your activities list, and see which activity type each would go under.

## Chapter 9: The Personal Essay

- [Essay Topic and Prompt Matcher](essay-prompt-matcher/index.md): Test a candidate essay topic against the qualities of a strong personal story, and see which Common App prompts it fits.
- [Show-Don't-Tell Practice](show-dont-tell-practice/index.md): Practice telling a general claim apart from a specific showing sentence, then write and check your own showing revision.
- [Personal Essay Structure Explorer](essay-structure-explorer/index.md): See how the parts of a personal essay work together under three common structures, and plan your own essay with a 650-word budget.

## Chapter 10: Supplemental Essays

- [Why Us Specificity Checker](why-us-specificity-checker/index.md): Test a Why Us essay draft for specificity: highlight generic phrases and specific details, and run the swap test with another college's name.
- [Supplement Planner](supplement-planner/index.md): Enter every supplemental essay prompt on your list, group them by type to see where one core essay can be adapted, and schedule the writing backward from deadlines.

## Chapter 11: Letters of Recommendation

- [Recommender Comparison Tool](recommender-comparison/index.md): Compare possible teacher recommenders on how well they know you, how recently they taught you, subject, and reliability, and see which two would write the strongest, most complementary letters.

## Chapter 12: Filling Out the Common App and Coalition App

- [Pre-Submission Checklist](pre-submission-checklist/index.md): A college-by-college review of your Common App application before you press Submit, so common errors are caught first.

## Chapter 13: Interviews

- [Interview Practice Deck](interview-practice-deck/index.md): Practice answering 24 common college interview questions out loud, with a timer, likely follow-ups, and a quick self-check.

## Chapter 14: Understanding Financial Aid

- [Financial Aid Map](financial-aid-map/index.md): An explorable map of where financial aid comes from (federal, state, institutional, private), the forms it takes, and which kinds are need-based or merit-based.
- [Need and Aid Calculator](need-and-aid-calculator/index.md): See how cost of attendance, the Student Aid Index, and a college's aid package combine into demonstrated need, net price, and unmet need.

## Chapter 15: Scholarships

- [Scholarship Priority Calculator](scholarship-priority-calculator/index.md): Rank scholarships by expected dollars per hour of work, see how renewability and reusing an essay change the ranking, and fit the best ones into the hours you have.
- [Scholarship or Scam?](scholarship-or-scam/index.md): Practice spotting scholarship scams by deciding whether twelve realistic offers look legitimate or like a scam, and naming the sign that decides it.

## Chapter 16: Comparing Award Letters

- [Award Letter Comparison Chart](award-comparison-chart/index.md): Compare up to four financial aid offers side by side on first-year net price, out-of-pocket cost, projected four-year cost, and debt.

## Chapter 17: Waiting and Managing Anxiety

## Chapter 18: Handling Decisions

## Chapter 19: Making Your Final Choice
