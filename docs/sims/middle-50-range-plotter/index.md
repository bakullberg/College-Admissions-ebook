## About This MicroSim

Help students interpret a college's middle 50% range and locate their own GPA and test score relative to it.

**Learning objective:** Students will interpret a college's middle 50% range for GPA and test scores and classify their own numbers as above, inside, or below the range.

This MicroSim supports [Chapter 2: How Colleges Evaluate Applicants](../../chapters/02-how-colleges-evaluate/index.md).

## How to Use

- Choose an example college to fill in its 25th and 75th percentiles, or type your own numbers from a college's Common Data Set or website.
- Enter **Your GPA** and **Your ACT**. The gold diamond marks your value and the label classifies it.
- Hover over a bar zone to see what share of admitted students fall there.
- Check **Test-optional college** to see why a test-optional range can overstate typical scores.

## Lesson Plan

### Learning Objective

Students will interpret a college's middle 50% range for GPA and test scores and classify their own numbers as above, inside, or below the range.

**Bloom level:** Analyze (L4) — interpret, classify

### Audience

High school juniors and seniors, and the parents, counselors, and mentors helping them.

### Duration

10 minutes

### Prerequisites

Read the section of [Chapter 2](../../chapters/02-how-colleges-evaluate/index.md) that introduces this MicroSim.

### Activities

1. **Read Emily's position (3 min):** With the defaults, explain what "inside range, lower half" means for Emily at Westbrook.
2. **Compare three colleges (4 min):** Switch among Westbrook, Lakeside State, and Harrow. Where does Emily fall at each?
3. **A real college (3 min):** Look up one college's middle 50% ranges and enter them with your own numbers.

### Discussion Questions

- What fraction of admitted students scored below the 25th percentile?
- Why might a test-optional college's reported ACT range be higher than its typical admitted student's score?
- Does being inside the range guarantee admission? Why not?

### Assessment

Students correctly classify a given GPA and test score against a stated middle 50% range and explain what each zone means.
