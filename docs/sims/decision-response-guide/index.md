## About This MicroSim

Give students a single interactive guide to the next steps for every kind of admission decision, including Early Decision cases, so no response or deadline is missed.

**Learning objective:** Students will select the decision they received and carry out the correct next steps, including deadlines, for acceptances, denials, deferrals, waitlists, and Early Decision outcomes.

This MicroSim supports [Chapter 18: Handling Decisions](../../chapters/18-handling-decisions/index.md).

## How to Use

- Click a decision to open its chain of steps, or click any step to read it. Gold steps are deadlines.
- Use **◀ Back** and **Next step ▶** to walk the chain, and check **Mark this step done** as you finish each one. Done steps turn green.
- Under **I have multiple decisions**, choose up to three decisions to see their chains side by side, with every deadline collected in one list.
- On narrow screens, the guide opens one decision at a time.

## Lesson Plan

### Learning Objective

Students will select the decision they received and carry out the correct next steps, including deadlines, for acceptances, denials, deferrals, waitlists, and Early Decision outcomes.

**Bloom level:** Apply (L3) — execute, respond

### Audience

High school juniors and seniors, and the parents, counselors, and mentors helping them.

### Duration

10 minutes, then as decisions arrive

### Prerequisites

Read the section of [Chapter 18](../../chapters/18-handling-decisions/index.md) that introduces this MicroSim.

### Activities

1. **Walk one chain (3 min):** Open Waitlist and read every step. Why should you deposit elsewhere by May 1 even if you hope to come off the waitlist?
2. **ED outcomes (3 min):** Compare the Early Decision acceptance chain with the Deferral chain. When are you released from the binding commitment?
3. **Your decisions (4 min):** As decisions arrive, choose them in the multiple-decision dropdowns and mark steps done.

### Discussion Questions

- What should you do first when an Early Decision offer isn't affordable?
- Why is depositing at two colleges a problem?
- How can you keep a denial in perspective?

### Assessment

Students list the correct next steps and deadline for three different decisions without the guide.
