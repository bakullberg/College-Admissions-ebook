## About This MicroSim

Let students calculate an unweighted GPA from their own courses and see how credits, individual grades, and core-only recalculation change the result.

**Learning objective:** Students will calculate an unweighted GPA from a list of courses and demonstrate how a college's core-only recalculation can produce a different number.

This MicroSim supports [Chapter 2: How Colleges Evaluate Applicants](../../chapters/02-how-colleges-evaluate/index.md).

## How to Use

- Each row is one course: type its name, pick a grade and credit value, and check the box if it is a core academic course.
- Both readouts update immediately: the GPA for all courses and the core-only GPA many colleges recalculate.
- **What moved it?** names the course whose last change shifted the GPA, and by how much.
- Use **Add course** and **Remove last** for up to eight rows, **Load Emily's year** to restore the chapter example (3.64 and 3.60), and **Show the math** to see the sums.

## Lesson Plan

### Learning Objective

Students will calculate an unweighted GPA from a list of courses and demonstrate how a college's core-only recalculation can produce a different number.

**Bloom level:** Apply (L3) — calculate, demonstrate

### Audience

High school juniors and seniors, and the parents, counselors, and mentors helping them.

### Duration

10–15 minutes

### Prerequisites

Read the section of [Chapter 2](../../chapters/02-how-colleges-evaluate/index.md) that introduces this MicroSim.

### Activities

1. **Check the example (3 min):** With Emily's year loaded, confirm 20.0 ÷ 5.5 = 3.64 and 18.0 ÷ 5.0 = 3.60 using Show the math.
2. **Credit weight (4 min):** Change Health from A to C, then change AP Chemistry from B to C. Which change moves the GPA more, and why?
3. **Your own year (6 min):** Enter your own courses from last year and compare your all-courses and core-only GPAs.

### Discussion Questions

- Why does a half-credit course move the GPA less than a full-credit one?
- Why might a college's core-only GPA differ from the one on your transcript?
- When could dropping electives raise a GPA, and when could it lower it?

### Assessment

Students calculate an unweighted GPA by hand for a new set of courses, match the calculator's result, and explain why the core-only GPA differs.
