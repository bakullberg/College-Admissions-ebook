## About This MicroSim

Let students compare their score against every college on their list and get a college-by-college submission recommendation, with the reasons shown.

**Learning objective:** Students will decide, college by college, whether to submit a test score by comparing it with each college's middle 50% range and policy, and justify each decision.

This MicroSim supports [Chapter 6: The SAT and ACT Question](../../chapters/06-sat-act-question/index.md).

## How to Use

- Enter your score and choose SAT or ACT. ACT scores are converted to SAT with the official concordance.
- Each row shows the college's middle 50% range (blue), its estimated midpoint (dark tick), any merit cutoff (green dashes), and your score (gold).
- Click a row to read the rule behind its badge and to edit that college's name, policy, range, merit cutoff, and inflated-range flag.
- Press **Try a retest score** and drag the slider to see which badges change. Changed badges flash gold. **Load Priya's list** restores the chapter's five colleges.

## Lesson Plan

### Learning Objective

Students will decide, college by college, whether to submit a test score by comparing it with each college's middle 50% range and policy, and justify each decision.

**Bloom level:** Evaluate (L5) — decide, justify

### Audience

High school juniors and seniors, and the parents, counselors, and mentors helping them.

### Duration

15 minutes

### Prerequisites

Read the section of [Chapter 6](../../chapters/06-sat-act-question/index.md) that introduces this MicroSim.

### Activities

1. **Check Priya's table (4 min):** With Priya's list loaded, confirm each badge matches the chapter's decision column.
2. **Retest (5 min):** Drag the retest slider. What score would turn College C into a Submit? Is that gain realistic?
3. **Your list (6 min):** Replace the colleges with your own, using each college's Common Data Set (Section C9) for the ranges.

### Discussion Questions

- Why make the submission decision college by college instead of once for your whole list?
- When does a merit-scholarship cutoff override the range comparison?
- Why might a test-optional college's published range overstate typical scores?

### Assessment

Students justify a submit, don't-submit, or judgment-call decision for three colleges using the score's position against each range.
