## About This MicroSim

Give students an interactive checklist of the five first steps and the chapter's setup tasks, showing progress and explaining why each task matters.

**Learning objective:** Students will carry out the five first steps and the core setup tasks of getting organized, tracking their progress toward a complete organizational system.

This MicroSim supports [Chapter 5: Getting Organized](../../chapters/05-getting-organized/index.md).

## How to Use

- Check off each task as you finish it. The ring shows your progress.
- Hover over a task to read why it matters and which chapter covers it.
- A 🔒 hint appears under a task whose prerequisite isn't done yet. You can still check it.
- Press **Show me what's next** to highlight your first unchecked task, or **Reset** to clear everything. Your checks are saved in this browser when storage is available.

## Lesson Plan

### Learning Objective

Students will carry out the five first steps and the core setup tasks of getting organized, tracking their progress toward a complete organizational system.

**Bloom level:** Apply (L3) — implement, carry out

### Audience

High school juniors and seniors, and the parents, counselors, and mentors helping them.

### Duration

5 minutes now, then ongoing

### Prerequisites

Read the section of [Chapter 5](../../chapters/05-getting-organized/index.md) that introduces this MicroSim.

### Activities

1. **Take stock (3 min):** Check every task you have already finished.
2. **Plan the week (5 min):** Press Show me what's next and schedule the next three tasks in your calendar.
3. **Family conversation (ongoing):** Use the Family roles discussed task to decide who handles forms, deadlines, and money conversations.

### Discussion Questions

- Why should the dedicated email come before the Common App account?
- Which task would cost you the most time if you skipped it?
- Who in your family will help with which tasks?

### Assessment

Students complete the five first steps and can explain the purpose of at least three foundation tasks.
