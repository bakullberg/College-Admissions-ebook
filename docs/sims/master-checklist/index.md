## About This MicroSim

Give students one place to track the whole admissions process, from spring of junior year to move-in, with visible progress and a real sense of reward as they go.

**Learning objective:** Students will carry out and track each step of the admissions process in order, deciding which steps apply to their own path.

This MicroSim supports the [Master Checklist](../../master-checklist.md) page and the month-by-month planner in [Chapter 20: Getting Ready for Fall](../../chapters/20-getting-ready-for-fall/index.md).

## How to Use

- Pick a stage from the menu, or use the arrows, and check off each step as you finish it.
- If a step isn't part of your path, check **Doesn't apply to me**. It counts as finished, so 100% is reachable on any path.
- Follow a **Chapter** link to read how to do that step.
- Press **Show me what's next** to jump to your first open step, or **Reset** to clear everything. Your checks are saved in this browser when storage is available.
- Finish a stage for confetti. Finish every stage for the full celebration.

## Lesson Plan

### Learning Objective

Students will carry out and track each step of the admissions process in order, deciding which steps apply to their own path.

**Bloom level:** Apply (L3) — implement, carry out

### Audience

High school juniors and seniors, and the parents, counselors, and mentors helping them.

### Duration

10 minutes now, then ongoing

### Prerequisites

None. This MicroSim is meant to be used from the start.

### Activities

1. **Take stock (5 min):** Check every step you've already finished, and mark the ones that don't apply to you.
2. **Plan the week (5 min):** Press Show me what's next and put the next three steps in your calendar.
3. **Monthly check-in (ongoing):** Open the checklist with a parent, guardian, or counselor once a month.

### Discussion Questions

- Which steps don't apply to your path, and how did you decide?
- Which stage looks hardest, and what could you start early to make it easier?
- Who can help you with the steps you're least sure about?

### Assessment

Students keep the checklist current and can explain why they marked any step as not applying to them.
