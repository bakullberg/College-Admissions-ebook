## About This MicroSim

Let students build a draft senior schedule and weekly commitments, then see both the rigor signal it sends and the weekly time load it creates, so they can find a balance before committing.

**Learning objective:** Students will judge whether a draft senior schedule balances rigor against workload by comparing its rigor signal with its estimated weekly hours, and adjust it to reach a sustainable plan.

This MicroSim supports [Chapter 7: Your Senior Year Course Load](../../chapters/07-senior-year-course-load/index.md).

## How to Use

- Choose a level for each of six courses and check the box for core academic subjects.
- Set your weekly job, activity, and application-work hours with the sliders.
- Read the two gauges (rigor signal and weekly load) and the feedback line that combines them.
- Load **Priya Draft 1**, **Priya Draft 2**, or **Marcus** to compare the chapter's schedules. **Reset** starts a regular schedule. Hour estimates are rough averages.

## Lesson Plan

### Learning Objective

Students will judge whether a draft senior schedule balances rigor against workload by comparing its rigor signal with its estimated weekly hours, and adjust it to reach a sustainable plan.

**Bloom level:** Evaluate (L5) — judge, balance

### Audience

High school juniors and seniors, and the parents, counselors, and mentors helping them.

### Duration

15 minutes

### Prerequisites

Read the section of [Chapter 7](../../chapters/07-senior-year-course-load/index.md) that introduces this MicroSim.

### Activities

1. **Compare Priya's drafts (5 min):** Load both drafts. What did Priya give up in Draft 2, and what did she gain?
2. **Marcus's week (4 min):** Load Marcus. Which single change brings his load below 50 hours while keeping his rigor signal?
3. **Your plan (6 min):** Enter your own senior schedule and real weekly hours. Adjust until the feedback is at least heavy but manageable.

### Discussion Questions

- Why can a 'very demanding' schedule be a stronger choice than the 'most demanding' one?
- What happens to the rigor signal when you drop a core subject?
- Where in your week do application hours come from?

### Assessment

Students propose a senior schedule that is at least demanding with a load under 50 hours and justify one trade-off they made.
