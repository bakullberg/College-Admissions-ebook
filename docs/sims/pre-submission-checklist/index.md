## About This MicroSim

Walk students through a complete, college-specific pre-submission review so that common errors (name mismatches, missing courses, wrong round, unassigned recommenders, truncated essays) are caught before submission.

**Learning objective:** Students will execute a structured pre-submission review of a Common App application for one college, checking every section and college-specific requirement before submitting.

This MicroSim supports [Chapter 12: Filling Out the Common App and Coalition App](../../chapters/12-common-app-coalition-app/index.md).

## How to Use

- Type the college's name and choose its round. Each college name keeps its own saved checklist in this browser.
- Check off each item as you verify it. Hover over an item to see why it matters.
- Check **This college requires Courses and Grades** if it does, to add that review.
- **Show only unchecked** hides finished items, and **Reset** clears the checklist. On narrow screens, use the group buttons to review one section at a time.

## Lesson Plan

### Learning Objective

Students will execute a structured pre-submission review of a Common App application for one college, checking every section and college-specific requirement before submitting.

**Bloom level:** Apply (L3) — execute, check

### Audience

High school juniors and seniors, and the parents, counselors, and mentors helping them.

### Duration

20–30 minutes per college

### Prerequisites

Read the section of [Chapter 12](../../chapters/12-common-app-coalition-app/index.md) that introduces this MicroSim.

### Activities

1. **One full review (20 min):** Review your first application with the checklist open beside the Common App preview.
2. **Find the risky items (3 min):** Which three items, if missed, would cause the biggest problems? Why?
3. **After submitting (2 min):** Confirm the confirmation email and the dashboard check for that college.

### Discussion Questions

- Why does selecting the wrong round matter so much?
- Why read the PDF preview instead of the form?
- What goes wrong if your counselor isn't assigned before you submit?

### Assessment

Students complete the checklist for one real or practice application and name the item they almost missed.
