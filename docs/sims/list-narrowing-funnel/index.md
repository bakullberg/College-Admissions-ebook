## About This MicroSim

Let students run a long college list through the three narrowing passes and see how each pass removes colleges for a specific reason, ending with a balanced final list.

**Learning objective:** Students will judge which colleges to cut from a long list at each narrowing pass and justify each cut using fit criteria and list balance.

This MicroSim supports [Chapter 4: Researching and Narrowing Your College List](../../chapters/04-researching-your-list/index.md).

## How to Use

- Press **Run Pass 1**, **Run Pass 2**, and **Run Pass 3** in order. Cut cards gray out, and the cut log records each reason.
- Card icons: R/T/S category, a dot for financial fit, a star for a strong CS program, and ✓ or ✗ for campus match. Hover over a card for details.
- After Pass 3, read the balance check on the final list.
- Check **Let me decide** to make the cuts yourself: choose a reason, click cards to cut them, then press **Finish Pass**. If you cut a college that fits every priority, you'll be asked to confirm.

## Lesson Plan

### Learning Objective

Students will judge which colleges to cut from a long list at each narrowing pass and justify each cut using fit criteria and list balance.

**Bloom level:** Evaluate (L5) — judge, justify

### Audience

High school juniors and seniors, and the parents, counselors, and mentors helping them.

### Duration

15 minutes

### Prerequisites

Read the section of [Chapter 4](../../chapters/04-researching-your-list/index.md) that introduces this MicroSim.

### Activities

1. **Watch the automatic passes (4 min):** Run all three passes and explain in one sentence why each pass cut what it did.
2. **Decide yourself (8 min):** Reset, check Let me decide, and narrow the list yourself. Compare your final list with the automatic one.
3. **Your own long list (3 min):** For each pass, name one college on your own list you might cut, and why.

### Discussion Questions

- Why does the balance pass come after the fit pass and not before?
- Is "I can't say why I want it" a good reason to cut a college? Why?
- What would you do if Pass 2 left you with only one safety?

### Assessment

Students narrow the long list to a final list that passes every balance check and justify each cut with a fit criterion or a balance reason.
