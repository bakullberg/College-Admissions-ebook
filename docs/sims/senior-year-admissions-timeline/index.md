## About This MicroSim

Show the shared calendar of the admissions process across one senior year, and how a student's choice of application rounds changes their personal workload.

**Learning objective:** Students will interpret a senior-year timeline to explain when applications, financial aid forms, decisions, and the May 1 commitment happen, and how applying early shifts the workload.

This MicroSim supports [Chapter 1: The College Landscape and Application Rounds](../../chapters/01-college-landscape-and-rounds/index.md).

## How to Use

- The top track shows the shared calendar every senior follows. May 1 is in red.
- Choose a plan from **Your plan** to swap the bottom track and read what changed in the caption.
- Click any item to read what it is and which chapter covers it.
- Drag the timeline sideways to pan, or use **+**, **−**, **◀**, **▶**, and **Show all**.

## Lesson Plan

### Learning Objective

Students will interpret a senior-year timeline to explain when applications, financial aid forms, decisions, and the May 1 commitment happen, and how applying early shifts the workload.

**Bloom level:** Understand (L2) — interpret

### Audience

High school juniors and seniors, and the parents, counselors, and mentors helping them.

### Duration

10 minutes

### Prerequisites

Read the section of [Chapter 1](../../chapters/01-college-landscape-and-rounds/index.md) that introduces this MicroSim.

### Activities

1. **Read the shared calendar (3 min):** Click every grey item and list the four dates every senior must know.
2. **Compare plans (4 min):** Switch between All Regular Decision and Early Action + Regular. Write down which months get busier.
3. **Early Decision outcomes (3 min):** Compare the two Early Decision options and explain why the deferred student needs Regular Decision applications almost finished by mid-December.

### Discussion Questions

- Which plan makes October the busiest month, and why?
- What does an Early Decision admit give up that an Early Action admit keeps?
- Why does May 1 matter in every plan, even the ones that end early?

### Assessment

Students correctly state when Regular Decision results arrive, what happens on May 1, and how an Early Action plan shifts work from January into October.
