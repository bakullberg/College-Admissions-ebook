## About This MicroSim

Let students build a complete, college-specific application roadmap, modeled on the chapter's Iowa capstone, that links every step of the process to its deadline and to the chapter that explains it.

**Learning objective:** Students will construct a complete application roadmap for one college on their list, filling in that college's specific policies, codes, and deadlines for every stage from list-building to move-in, and verifying each against the college's own website.

This MicroSim supports [Chapter 20: Getting Ready for Fall](../../chapters/20-getting-ready-for-fall/index.md).

## How to Use

- Type a **College name** and choose where you are **Starting from**. If you're starting late, the roadmap points out the stops you need to catch up on.
- For each stop, enter this college's specifics (policies, codes, cutoffs) and a date if it has one. Check **Verified** once you've confirmed it on the college's website. Unverified stops show an orange *verify* tag on the road.
- Click a road stop to jump to its page of entries, or use **Earlier stops** and **Later stops**. The **Dates in order** panel lists every date you entered, with overdue dates in red.
- **Load Iowa (Casey)** fills in the chapter's capstone example. **Blank roadmap** starts fresh. Each college's roadmap is saved in this browser under its name, so you can keep several. **Print / save as PDF** prints a clean copy.

## Lesson Plan

### Learning Objective

Students will construct a complete application roadmap for one college on their list, filling in that college's specific policies, codes, and deadlines for every stage from list-building to move-in, and verifying each against the college's own website.

**Bloom level:** Create (L6) — construct, plan

### Audience

High school juniors and seniors, and the parents, counselors, and mentors helping them.

### Duration

15 minutes in class, then ongoing through senior year

### Prerequisites

Read the section of [Chapter 20](../../chapters/20-getting-ready-for-fall/index.md) that introduces this MicroSim.

### Activities

1. **Model (3 min):** Load Iowa (Casey). Read the 19 stops in order and find the three earliest dates.
2. **Build (10 min):** Start a blank roadmap for one college on your list. Fill in at least the first six stops from that college's website.
3. **Verify (2 min):** Check Verified only for stops you confirmed on the college's own site. How many are still orange?
4. **Extend (homework):** Finish all 19 stops, then build a second roadmap for a college with a different deadline structure.

### Discussion Questions

- Which stops on your roadmap have hard deadlines, and which are ongoing?
- Why does every stop need to be verified on the college's own website rather than a third-party site?
- How would your roadmap change if you were starting in the fall of senior year instead of junior spring?

### Assessment

Students submit a printed roadmap for one college with all 19 stops filled, every date verified, and at least one catch-up decision explained.
