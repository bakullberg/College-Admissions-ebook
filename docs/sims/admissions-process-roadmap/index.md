## About This MicroSim

Give students a single picture of the six stages of the college admissions process and let them explore each stage on demand.

**Learning objective:** Students will identify the six stages of the college admissions process in order and recall roughly when each one happens in senior year.

This MicroSim supports [Chapter 1: The College Landscape and Application Rounds](../../chapters/01-college-landscape-and-rounds/index.md).

## How to Use

- Hover over a stop to see its stage name and months.
- Click a stop to open its infobox: what you do, when, and which chapters cover it.
- Press **Quiz me** to hide the labels. Drag each stage tile onto the correct stop. Correct drops turn green; wrong drops bounce back.
- Press **Show answers** to reveal every label.

## Lesson Plan

### Learning Objective

Students will identify the six stages of the college admissions process in order and recall roughly when each one happens in senior year.

**Bloom level:** Remember (L1) — identify, sequence

### Audience

High school juniors and seniors, and the parents, counselors, and mentors helping them.

### Duration

5–10 minutes

### Prerequisites

Read the section of [Chapter 1](../../chapters/01-college-landscape-and-rounds/index.md) that introduces this MicroSim.

### Activities

1. **Explore (3 min):** Click each of the six stops and note the months for each stage.
2. **Quiz (3 min):** Press Quiz me and place all six tiles without help.
3. **Personalize (4 min):** Write today's date on a sheet of paper and mark which stage you are in and which stage comes next.

### Discussion Questions

- Which stages overlap in time? What does that mean for your fall schedule?
- Why does Build Your List come before Apply, even though some applications open in August?
- Which stage happens after you're admitted, and what is its key deadline?

### Assessment

Students place all six tiles correctly in quiz mode and can name the month of the national commitment deadline (May 1).
