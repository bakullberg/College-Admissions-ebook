## About This MicroSim

Let students run a real formula-based admissions index and see how each input, and residency, affects whether they clear the threshold.

**Learning objective:** Students will use the Regent Admission Index formula to calculate a score from GPA, ACT, and core-course years and determine whether it meets the resident and nonresident thresholds.

This MicroSim supports [Chapter 2: How Colleges Evaluate Applicants](../../chapters/02-how-colleges-evaluate/index.md).

## How to Use

- Move the **Cumulative GPA**, **ACT composite**, and **Years of core courses** sliders. The stacked bar shows how many points each input contributes.
- Choose **Iowa resident** or **Nonresident** to highlight the threshold that applies (245 or 255).
- Read the status lines to see how far above or below each threshold you are.
- Press **Casey (ACT 24)** or **Casey (ACT 22)** for the chapter's worked example, and **Show formula** to see the arithmetic.
- Hover over a bar segment to see how much one more unit of that input adds.

## Lesson Plan

### Learning Objective

Students will use the Regent Admission Index formula to calculate a score from GPA, ACT, and core-course years and determine whether it meets the resident and nonresident thresholds.

**Bloom level:** Apply (L3) — calculate, use

### Audience

High school juniors and seniors, and the parents, counselors, and mentors helping them.

### Duration

10 minutes

### Prerequisites

Read the section of [Chapter 2](../../chapters/02-how-colleges-evaluate/index.md) that introduces this MicroSim.

### Activities

1. **Check Casey (3 min):** Load both Casey presets and confirm 259 and 253 by hand.
2. **Which input moves most? (4 min):** From Casey (ACT 22), find three different single changes that bring the RAI to 255.
3. **Residency (3 min):** Find a score that clears the resident threshold but not the nonresident one, and explain what that means for an out-of-state applicant.

### Discussion Questions

- A full GPA point is worth 30 RAI points and an ACT point is worth 3. What does that weighting say about what Iowa values?
- Why can one more year of a core course matter as much as almost two ACT points?
- How does Iowa review an applicant who doesn't submit a test score?

### Assessment

Students calculate an RAI by hand from three inputs and state whether it clears the resident and nonresident thresholds.
