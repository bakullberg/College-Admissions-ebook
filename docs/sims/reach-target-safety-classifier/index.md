## About This MicroSim

Let students classify a college as a reach, target, or safety using their own numbers, the college's middle 50% range and admission rate, and financial fit.

**Learning objective:** Students will apply admission-rate, middle 50% range, and affordability criteria to classify colleges as reach, target, or safety schools for themselves.

This MicroSim supports [Chapter 3: Finding Your Fit](../../chapters/03-finding-your-fit/index.md).

## How to Use

- Enter your GPA and test score (choose SAT or ACT), then the college's admission rate and 25th–75th percentile ranges.
- Choose the college's **Financial fit** and check **I'd be glad to attend** if that's true.
- The number lines show your values (gold) against the college's middle 50% (blue). The badge gives the category and the deciding factors.
- Hover over the badge to see which rule fired. Try the four preset buttons for the chapter's examples.

## Lesson Plan

### Learning Objective

Students will apply admission-rate, middle 50% range, and affordability criteria to classify colleges as reach, target, or safety schools for themselves.

**Bloom level:** Apply (L3) — classify, apply

### Audience

High school juniors and seniors, and the parents, counselors, and mentors helping them.

### Duration

10–15 minutes

### Prerequisites

Read the section of [Chapter 3](../../chapters/03-finding-your-fit/index.md) that introduces this MicroSim.

### Activities

1. **Run the presets (4 min):** Load all four and explain the deciding rule for each.
2. **Find the edge (4 min):** From Priya → College C, change only the admission rate. At what rate does it become 'Target leaning reach'? What happens below 10%?
3. **Your list (7 min):** Classify three colleges you are considering using their real numbers.

### Discussion Questions

- Why is a college with a 6% admission rate a reach even for a student with top scores?
- Why isn't a likely admission enough to make a college a safety?
- What would you need to check before calling a college affordable?

### Assessment

Students classify three colleges correctly using the five rules and name the deciding rule for each.
