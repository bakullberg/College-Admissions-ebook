## About This MicroSim

Give students a single, explorable picture of where financial aid comes from, what forms it takes, and which kinds depend on need versus merit.

**Learning objective:** Students will classify types of financial aid by source (federal, state, institutional, private), form (grant, scholarship, loan, work-study), and basis (need or merit), and explain which kinds must be repaid.

This MicroSim supports [Chapter 14: Understanding Financial Aid](../../chapters/14-understanding-financial-aid/index.md).

## How to Use

- Each chip is a real kind of aid. Its column is the source and its row is the form. Blue = need-based, gold = merit, blue-and-gold = either, gray = not based on need.
- Grants and scholarships are gift aid (never repaid). Loans and work-study are self-help aid.
- Click any chip to read what it is and what unlocks it. Hover over a source header for a one-line description.
- Use the filter buttons to show only gift aid, only need-based aid, or only what the FAFSA unlocks.

## Lesson Plan

### Learning Objective

Students will classify types of financial aid by source (federal, state, institutional, private), form (grant, scholarship, loan, work-study), and basis (need or merit), and explain which kinds must be repaid.

**Bloom level:** Understand (L2) — classify, explain

### Audience

High school juniors and seniors, and the parents, counselors, and mentors helping them.

### Duration

10 minutes

### Prerequisites

Read the section of [Chapter 14](../../chapters/14-understanding-financial-aid/index.md) that introduces this MicroSim.

### Activities

1. **The FAFSA's reach (3 min):** Press Show only what the FAFSA unlocks. How much of the map does one form open?
2. **Gift vs. self-help (3 min):** Press Show only gift aid. Which sources give gift aid in more than one form?
3. **Your plan (4 min):** List the chips you could realistically receive and what you must file to get each one.

### Discussion Questions

- Why is a dollar of grant aid worth more than a dollar of loan aid?
- Which aid can you get only by filling out a separate application?
- Why should a family file the FAFSA even if they don't expect need-based aid?

### Assessment

Students classify six aid examples by source, form, and basis, and correctly say which must be repaid.
