## About This MicroSim

Let students see the difference between a deep activity profile and a broad one by plotting each activity's years of involvement against its weekly hours, then plot their own activities.

**Learning objective:** Students will compare activity profiles by years of involvement and weekly hours, and distinguish a profile that shows depth from one that shows breadth alone.

This MicroSim supports [Chapter 8: Activities and Resume](../../chapters/08-activities-and-resume/index.md).

## How to Use

- Each bubble is one activity: farther right = more years, higher = more hours per week, bigger = more hours per year. Gold = a leadership or growing role.
- Use **Show Applicant A**, **Show Applicant B**, or **Show both** to compare the chapter's two profiles.
- Add up to ten of your own activities with the form. They appear as blue outlines. Click one of your bubbles to edit it, or **Clear mine** to start over.
- Read the summary lines for how many activities fall in the shaded depth zone (3+ years and 5+ hours a week).

## Lesson Plan

### Learning Objective

Students will compare activity profiles by years of involvement and weekly hours, and distinguish a profile that shows depth from one that shows breadth alone.

**Bloom level:** Analyze (L4) — compare, distinguish

### Audience

High school juniors and seniors, and the parents, counselors, and mentors helping them.

### Duration

10–15 minutes

### Prerequisites

Read the section of [Chapter 8](../../chapters/08-activities-and-resume/index.md) that introduces this MicroSim.

### Activities

1. **Compare profiles (4 min):** Applicant A lists ten activities and Applicant B four. Which shows more total time? Which shows more depth?
2. **Plot yourself (6 min):** Add your own activities with honest hours. How many land in the depth zone?
3. **Plan senior year (3 min):** Pick one activity you could move toward the depth zone or into a growing role this year.

### Discussion Questions

- Why can four activities tell a reader more than ten?
- Does an activity have to be in the depth zone to be worth listing?
- What counts as a 'growing role' if you don't have a title?

### Assessment

Students distinguish a deep profile from a broad one using years, hours, and role, and identify one of their own activities with the most depth.
