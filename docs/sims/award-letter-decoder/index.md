## About This MicroSim

Teach students to translate any award letter into standard numbers by sorting each line into free, earned, or borrowed money and computing net price, out-of-pocket cost, and first-year borrowing.

**Learning objective:** Students will classify each line of an award letter as free, earned, or borrowed money and calculate the offer's net price and out-of-pocket cost, ignoring misleading letter totals.

This MicroSim supports [Chapter 16: Comparing Award Letters](../../chapters/16-comparing-award-letters/index.md).

## How to Use

- Choose a letter: **Lakeshore**, **College C**, or **Hillcrest**. Drag each aid line into Free money, Earned money, or Borrowed money. On narrow screens, tap a line and then tap a zone.
- Wrong drops bounce back with a hint. Once every line is sorted, compare the letter's remaining balance with your real net price.
- If the cost of attendance is missing (as in College C's letter), look it up and type it in ($64,000 for College C).
- Use the checkboxes to accept or decline work-study and student loans, and hover over any number to see its formula. **Enter my own** lets you type in a letter of your own, up to eight lines.

## Lesson Plan

### Learning Objective

Students will classify each line of an award letter as free, earned, or borrowed money and calculate the offer's net price and out-of-pocket cost, ignoring misleading letter totals.

**Bloom level:** Apply (L3) — classify, calculate

### Audience

High school juniors and seniors, and the parents, counselors, and mentors helping them.

### Duration

15 minutes

### Prerequisites

Read the section of [Chapter 16](../../chapters/16-comparing-award-letters/index.md) that introduces this MicroSim.

### Activities

1. **Lakeshore (5 min):** Sort the letter and explain why the $6,000 remaining balance is misleading.
2. **The missing cost (4 min):** Load College C. What's the net price once you enter the published cost?
3. **Your letter (6 min):** When you receive an award letter, enter it with Enter my own.

### Discussion Questions

- Why isn't a Parent PLUS loan financial aid?
- What is the difference between net price and out-of-pocket cost?
- What should you do when a letter leaves out the cost of attendance?

### Assessment

Students sort a new award letter correctly and calculate its net price and out-of-pocket cost.
