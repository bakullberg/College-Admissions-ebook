## About This MicroSim

Let students draft a Common App-style activity entry within the real character limits and get feedback on action verbs, specifics, and measurable impact.

**Learning objective:** Students will compose an activity entry within the Common App's character limits and revise its description to lead with an action verb and include specific, measurable detail.

This MicroSim supports [Chapter 8: Activities and Resume](../../chapters/08-activities-and-resume/index.md).

## How to Use

- Fill in the activity type, grades, timing, hours, position, organization, and description. Counters show the Common App limits (50, 100, and 150 characters) and turn orange near the limit and red over it.
- Watch the **Reader's view** card update. Text beyond 150 characters appears struck through in red.
- Read the **Feedback** panel: an action verb first, a number, no "I" or filler, no repeated position, and realistic hours, plus total hours per year.
- Load a preset to compare the chapter's entries. **Sam — tutoring before** and **after** show a weak draft and its revision. **Copy entry text** copies your entry.

## Lesson Plan

### Learning Objective

Students will compose an activity entry within the Common App's character limits and revise its description to lead with an action verb and include specific, measurable detail.

**Bloom level:** Create (L6) — compose, revise

### Audience

High school juniors and seniors, and the parents, counselors, and mentors helping them.

### Duration

15–20 minutes

### Prerequisites

Read the section of [Chapter 8](../../chapters/08-activities-and-resume/index.md) that introduces this MicroSim.

### Activities

1. **Before and after (4 min):** Load Sam's two drafts and list every check the revision fixes.
2. **Revise Sam yourself (5 min):** From the before draft, write your own revision before loading the after version. Compare.
3. **Your best activity (8 min):** Write the entry for your strongest activity until every check passes.

### Discussion Questions

- Why does the first word of a description matter so much?
- What kinds of numbers can make an activity concrete?
- What should you cut first when a description runs over 150 characters?

### Assessment

Students write an entry that passes every check, stays within all character limits, and shows an action, a specific detail, and a result.
