## About This MicroSim

Help students test a Why Us draft for specificity by running the swap test and highlighting generic phrases, so they can see where research and personal connection are missing.

**Learning objective:** Students will critique a Why Us essay draft by applying the swap test and assessing whether each sentence names a specific college detail and connects it to the writer.

This MicroSim supports [Chapter 10: Supplemental Essays](../../chapters/10-supplemental-essays/index.md).

## How to Use

- Enter the college's name and a different college's name, then paste your draft.
- Press **Check**. Orange sentences contain generic phrases, green sentences contain a likely specific detail, and a blue underline marks a detail connected to you.
- Press **Swap test** to see your draft with the other college's name. If it still makes sense, it isn't specific enough.
- Load **Priya — first draft** and **Priya — revision** to compare the chapter's example. The checker uses simple patterns and can't judge quality.

## Lesson Plan

### Learning Objective

Students will critique a Why Us essay draft by applying the swap test and assessing whether each sentence names a specific college detail and connects it to the writer.

**Bloom level:** Evaluate (L5) — critique, assess

### Audience

High school juniors and seniors, and the parents, counselors, and mentors helping them.

### Duration

10–15 minutes

### Prerequisites

Read the section of [Chapter 10](../../chapters/10-supplemental-essays/index.md) that introduces this MicroSim.

### Activities

1. **Priya's two drafts (4 min):** Check both drafts. Which details in the revision would be impossible to write about another college?
2. **Swap your own (5 min):** Paste one of your Why Us drafts and run the swap test.
3. **Add two details (6 min):** For each orange sentence, replace the generic phrase with a specific course, program, place, or person, and say why it matters to you.

### Discussion Questions

- What makes a detail specific to one college?
- Why isn't a specific detail enough on its own without a connection to you?
- Where can you find details that most applicants won't mention?

### Assessment

Students revise a Why Us draft until it fails the swap test and every specific detail connects to them.
