# Concept Taxonomy

The 342 concepts in the College Admissions Roadmap learning graph are grouped into 13 categories. Each category has a short TaxonomyID used in `learning-graph.csv` and the graph viewer legend.

| Category | TaxonomyID | Concepts | What belongs here |
|---|---|:---:|---|
| Admissions Foundations | FOUND | 39 | How selective admissions works: holistic and formula-based review, GPA and course rigor, types of colleges, residency, application rounds (ED/EA/RD), and the common myths. |
| College List Building | LIST | 31 | Self-assessment, the dimensions of fit, reach/target/safety, research tools and data sources, campus visits, and narrowing to a final list. |
| Organization and Planning | ORG | 28 | Deadlines, trackers, filing systems, accounts and portals, counselors and family roles, the senior-year calendar, fees, and the capstone roadmap. |
| Testing | TEST | 25 | The SAT and ACT, test-optional/required/blind policies, the score-submission decision, superscoring, retesting, registration, and low-cost prep. |
| Senior Year Academics | ACAD | 17 | Senior course load and grades, mid-year and final transcripts, AP, IB, dual enrollment, and balancing rigor against burnout. |
| Activities and Resume | ACTV | 17 | Extracurriculars, jobs, and family responsibilities; depth, commitment, and impact; and building the activities list and resume. |
| Essays | ESSAY | 30 | The personal essay from brainstorming through revision, and supplemental essays including Why Us, major, community, and short answers. |
| Recommendations and Interviews | RECS | 22 | Choosing, asking, and supporting recommenders, and preparing for alumni, admissions, and virtual interviews. |
| Application Platforms | PLAT | 25 | The Common App, Coalition App, and institutional applications, section by section, through submission, transcripts, and score sending. |
| Financial Aid | AID | 37 | Cost of attendance and net price, the FAFSA and CSS Profile, types of aid, need-blind vs. need-aware, grants, work-study, and loans. |
| Scholarships and Award Letters | SCHOL | 27 | Finding and prioritizing scholarships, avoiding scams, reading and comparing award letters, and appealing aid offers. |
| Decisions and Enrollment | DEC | 33 | Application review, decisions, waitlists, comparing offers, committing by May 1, and every step of getting ready for fall. |
| Wellbeing and Mindset | WELL | 11 | Managing stress and uncertainty through the process, reframing rejection, and building healthy habits and support. |
