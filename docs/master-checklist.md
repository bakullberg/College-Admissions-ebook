## How It Works

- **Not every step is for everyone.** If you aren't testing, applying early, or filing a particular form, check **Doesn't apply to me**. It counts as finished, so you can reach 100% on your own path.
- **Starting late is fine.** Mark what you've already done, then press **Show me what's next** to find your first open step.
- **Stuck on a step?** Each one links to the chapter that explains how to do it.
- **Your progress stays in this browser.** It isn't sent anywhere, so it won't follow you to another device, and clearing your browser data will erase it.

The months here are typical, so always confirm the real deadlines for each college on your list. The [month-by-month planner in Chapter 20](chapters/20-getting-ready-for-fall/index.md#the-month-by-month-planner) shows the same plan as a single table, which is handy for printing.
