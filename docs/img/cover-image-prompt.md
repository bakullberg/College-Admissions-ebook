# Cover Image Prompt

Paste everything in the box below into ChatGPT, Gemini or another image tool. Generate two to four versions, pick the best, and save it as `docs/img/cover.png`, replacing the current generic cover. If the tool lets you attach a reference image, attach Scout's final `docs/img/mascot/welcome.png` once it exists.

```text
Please generate a professional-quality cover image for this textbook.
This image will be used in social media previews and must follow the
formatting guidelines for an Open Graph image preview.

Required specifications:
- Format: PNG
- Wide-landscape format
- Size: 1200x630 pixels (1.91:1 aspect ratio)
- This is the Open Graph standard for social media previews

The image has four layers, back to front: background montage, color
treatment, mascot, and title text.

SUBJECT & TONE
"College Admissions Roadmap" is a free, interactive guide that walks high
school students step by step through applying to college, from building a
college list to financial aid, decisions and move-in day. The audience is
high school seniors, many of them the first in their family to apply, plus
their parents and counselors. The tone should be warm, calm and
encouraging: organized and hopeful, never stressful or frantic.

TITLE
Place "College Admissions Roadmap" in the center of the image in a clean,
bold, highly legible sans-serif font. Use white text with a subtle drop
shadow or a soft dark indigo scrim behind it so it stays readable against
the background. Keep the title large; simplify the background directly
behind it rather than shrinking the text. No other text anywhere in the
image.

BACKGROUND MONTAGE
Behind and around the title, draw a winding road that runs across the whole
image like a route on a map, with small milestone pins along it. Arrange
these eight elements along and around the road, all in the same flat
illustration style:
1. College list: a clipboard with a short checklist of colleges, some
   items checked off.
2. Deadlines: a wall calendar page with a few dates circled in orange.
3. Testing: a pencil resting on a bubble-sheet answer card.
4. The application: an open laptop showing a simple form with checkboxes
   and a progress bar.
5. The essay: a sheet of paper with handwritten lines and a few gentle
   orange revision marks.
6. Paying for college: a simple bar chart comparing two award letters, with
   segments for grants and loans.
7. Decision day: an opened envelope with a letter and a small burst of
   confetti.
8. Move-in day: a cardboard box with a desk lamp and a pillow sticking
   out, in front of a campus building with a clock tower and trees.

MASCOT
Place the book's mascot, Scout the Turtle, in the lower-left corner, as if
walking along the start of the road, sized so it doesn't overlap the
title. Scout is a small, friendly cartoon turtle standing upright on the
back legs, with soft sea green skin (#6BAF92), an indigo shell (#3F51B5)
with lighter indigo hexagon plates, a loosely knotted orange scarf
(#FF9800), and a small round brass compass on a cord around the neck. Scout
has calm, warm eyes and a gentle smile and is waving hello.

STYLE & COMPOSITION
- Illustration style: modern flat vector with clean lines and solid colors,
  applied to every element so the image reads as one picture.
- Color palette: deep indigo (#3F51B5) and warm orange (#FF9800), with soft
  sea green and cream accents, on a light-to-mid indigo background.
- Lighting and mood: bright, calm and optimistic, like early morning.
- Composition: title centered; the road and montage elements in a loose
  ring around it; Scout in the lower left; plenty of open space directly
  behind the title.

AVOID
- No dense or readable paragraphs of text, no college names, logos or
  brand names.
- No stock-photo cliches like handshakes, lightbulbs or people pointing at
  whiteboards.
- No photorealistic human faces.
- Do not let any montage element overlap or crowd the title.
```
