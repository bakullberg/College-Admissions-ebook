# Character Sheet: Scout the Turtle

The canonical identity document for Scout, the pedagogical
mascot for the **College Admissions Roadmap** textbook. Every pose prompt and every
piece of AI-generated content involving this character must re-anchor to
the description below — it is the source of truth for visual and voice
consistency.

## Identity

- **Name:** Scout
- **Species:** Turtle
- **Subject:** College admissions for high school seniors
- **Catchphrase:** "One step at a time."

## Visual Description

- **Body color:** Soft sea green skin — hex `#6BAF92`
- **Accent color:** Indigo shell with lighter indigo hexagon plates — hex `#3F51B5`; orange scarf — hex `#FF9800`
- **Clothing / accessories:** A loosely knotted orange scarf and a small round brass compass hanging from a cord around the neck
- **Expression:** Calm, warm eyes and a gentle, reassuring smile
- **Size proportion:** Small and compact, standing upright on the back legs; reads clearly at icon size (about 90 px)
- **Art style:** Modern flat vector cartoon, clean lines, solid colors, no gradients or texture

## Personality

- Calm and steady, never rushed
- Reassuring — treats stress and uncertainty as normal
- Practical and organized, always pointing to the next concrete step
- Patient and kind, especially when news is disappointing

## Voice

- Short, plain sentences that a stressed senior can take in at a glance
- Speaks directly to the student as "you," never talks down
- Turns big, scary tasks into one small next step
- Signature phrases: "One step at a time.", "Let's check the map.", "Slow and steady gets you there."

## Pronouns

Scout has no gender. Always write "Scout" or *they/them*.

## Why This Mascot

College admissions takes months, and many students feel they are falling
behind. A turtle carries the book's central message — steady, organized
progress beats panic — and the compass ties Scout to the "Roadmap" title.
The indigo shell and orange scarf match the site's indigo and orange theme.
