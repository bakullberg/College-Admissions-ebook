# References: Comparing Award Letters

1. [Federal Direct Student Loan Program](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_Direct_Student_Loan_Program) - Wikipedia - Explains federal Direct Loans, including subsidized and unsubsidized loans, borrowing limits and repayment, the loans most often listed on award letters.

2. [PLUS Loan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PLUS_Loan) - Wikipedia - Describes federal PLUS loans for parents and graduate students, including credit requirements and costs, background for the chapter's Parent PLUS section.

3. [Federal Work-Study Program](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_Work-Study_Program) - Wikipedia - Explains how federal work-study jobs are funded and paid, supporting the chapter's point that work-study is earned during the year, not subtracted from the bill.

4. Paying the Price: College Costs, Financial Aid, and the Betrayal of the American Dream - Sara Goldrick-Rab - University of Chicago Press (2016) - Goldrick-Rab followed thousands of Wisconsin students and showed how aid packages often leave large gaps once housing, food and books are counted, the reason this chapter focuses on real out-of-pocket cost.

5. The Price You Pay for College - Ron Lieber - Harper (2021) - The New York Times money columnist explains how colleges use merit aid and tuition discounting to compete for students, and reframes the family question from "what will it cost?" to "what is it worth to us?"

6. [Subsidized and Unsubsidized Loans](https://studentaid.gov/understand-aid/types/loans/subsidized-unsubsidized) - Federal Student Aid - Official comparison of subsidized and unsubsidized Direct Loans, including eligibility, interest and annual limits. Use it to identify which loans on an award letter build interest during college.

7. [Parent PLUS Loans](https://studentaid.gov/understand-aid/types/loans/plus/parent) - Federal Student Aid - Official explanation of Parent PLUS loan eligibility, interest rates and repayment, useful for judging any PLUS loan on an award letter.

8. [Understanding Your Financial Aid Award Offers](https://bigfuture.collegeboard.org/pay-for-college/get-help-paying-for-college/scholarships-grants-institutional-aid/understanding-your-financial-aid-award-offers) - College Board BigFuture - Line-by-line guide to reading an award letter and separating grants from loans, a companion to the chapter's Award Letter Decoder MicroSim.

9. [How to Review and Compare Financial Aid Awards](https://bigfuture.collegeboard.org/pay-for-college/get-help-paying-for-college/scholarships-grants-institutional-aid/how-review-compare-financial-aid-awards) - College Board BigFuture - Step-by-step method for comparing offers side by side, matching the chapter's award letter comparison section. Includes a worksheet for putting every offer in the same format.

10. [Paying for College](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/paying-for-college/) - Consumer Financial Protection Bureau - Federal tools for comparing costs, understanding loans and estimating debt at graduation. Its estimates make the chapter's four-year cost projection easier to build.
