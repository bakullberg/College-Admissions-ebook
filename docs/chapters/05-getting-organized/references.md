# References: Getting Organized

1. [Common App](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Common_App) - Wikipedia - History and structure of the Common Application, the shared platform most students use, including how member colleges add their own questions and supplements.

2. [School counselor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/School_counselor) - Wikipedia - Describes the roles of school counselors, including college advising, transcripts and recommendation letters, background for the chapter's section on the people who help.

3. [Educational consultant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Educational_consultant) - Wikipedia - Explains what independent educational consultants do and how they differ from school counselors, supporting the chapter's discussion of independent counselors.

4. College Admission: From Application to Acceptance, Step by Step - Robin Mamlet and Christine VanDeVelde - Three Rivers Press (2011) - Mamlet, former dean of admission at Stanford, Swarthmore and Sarah Lawrence, built this step-by-step guide on interviews with deans and counselors, organizing the whole process around a senior-year calendar of tasks.

5. Getting Things Done: The Art of Stress-Free Productivity - David Allen - Penguin Books (2001) - Allen's idea of a "trusted system" that captures every task and next action outside your head is the principle behind the chapter's deadline tracker, checklists and calendar reminders.

6. [College Application Checklist](https://bigfuture.collegeboard.org/plan-for-college/apply-to-college/application-process/college-application-checklist) - College Board BigFuture - Printable checklist of every application component and deadline, a ready-made starting point for the application checklist this chapter describes. Copy its items into your own tracker.

7. [College Application Timeline: 12th Grade](https://bigfuture.collegeboard.org/plan-for-college/apply-to-college/college-application-timeline-12th-grade) - College Board BigFuture - Month-by-month senior-year timeline of applications, testing and aid, matching the senior year calendar laid out at the end of this chapter.

8. [Your High School Counselor's Role](https://bigfuture.collegeboard.org/plan-for-college/apply-to-college/your-high-school-counselors-role) - College Board BigFuture - Explains what school counselors do in the application process and how to work with them, supporting the chapter's counselor meeting section.

9. [20 Questions to Ask Your School Counselor](https://bigfuture.collegeboard.org/plan-for-college/apply-to-college/20-questions-to-ask-your-school-counselor) - College Board BigFuture - Ready-made list of questions for the counselor meeting, from course selection to deadlines and recommendations. Bring it to your first senior-year meeting.

10. [Applying as a First-Year Student](https://www.commonapp.org/apply/first-year-students) - Common App - The Common App's official guide to creating an account, adding colleges and completing each section, the starting point for one of the chapter's five first steps.
