# References: Letters of Recommendation

1. [Letter of recommendation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Letter_of_recommendation) - Wikipedia - Explains the purpose and typical content of recommendation letters in admissions and hiring, background for the chapter's discussion of teacher and counselor letters.

2. [Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Family_Educational_Rights_and_Privacy_Act) - Wikipedia - Describes the federal law behind the FERPA waiver on the Common App, including students' rights to see their education records.

3. [School counselor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/School_counselor) - Wikipedia - Covers the counselor's role, including writing the counselor recommendation and sending the school profile and transcript. It also explains what a school profile tells colleges.

4. Admissions Confidential: An Insider's Account of the Elite College Selection Process - Rachel Toor - St. Martin's Press (2001) - A former Duke admissions officer's candid account of reading thousands of files, showing how quickly readers judge transcripts, essays and recommendations, and what makes a letter or essay stand out from the stack.

5. College Admission: From Application to Acceptance, Step by Step - Robin Mamlet and Christine VanDeVelde - Three Rivers Press (2011) - Mamlet, former dean of admission at Stanford, Swarthmore and Sarah Lawrence, built this step-by-step guide on interviews with deans and counselors, organizing the whole process around a senior-year calendar of tasks.

6. [How to Get a Great Letter of Recommendation](https://bigfuture.collegeboard.org/plan-for-college/apply-to-college/application-process/how-to-get-a-great-letter-of-recommendation) - College Board BigFuture - Guidance on choosing recommenders, asking early and giving them the information they need, matching the chapter's brag sheet and recommender packet sections.

7. [Counselors and Recommenders](https://www.commonapp.org/counselors-and-recommenders) - Common App - The Common App's resources for teachers and counselors, showing how recommenders receive invitations and submit letters. Share it with recommenders who haven't used the Common App before.

8. [Common App Applicant Help Center](https://appsupport.commonapp.org/applicantsupport/s/) - Common App - Searchable help articles on inviting recommenders, the FERPA release and tracking whether letters have been submitted. Use it when a letter still shows as not submitted near a deadline.

9. [FERPA](https://studentprivacy.ed.gov/ferpa) - U.S. Department of Education - Official explanation of FERPA and student privacy rights, useful for understanding exactly what the Common App's FERPA waiver gives up.

10. [Your High School Counselor's Role](https://bigfuture.collegeboard.org/plan-for-college/apply-to-college/your-high-school-counselors-role) - College Board BigFuture - Explains what the counselor letter and school report include and how to help your counselor write a strong recommendation. Share it with your counselor early.
