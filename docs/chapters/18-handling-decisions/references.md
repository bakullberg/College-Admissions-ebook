# References: Handling Decisions

1. [Wait list](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wait_list) - Wikipedia - Explains how waitlists work in college admission and other settings, background for the chapter's waitlist and waitlist strategy sections. It explains how colleges admit from them.

2. [Yield (college admissions)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yield_(college_admissions)) - Wikipedia - Describes how colleges predict and manage yield, which explains deferrals, waitlists and the value of a letter of continued interest.

3. [Social rejection](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Social_rejection) - Wikipedia - Covers the psychology of rejection and how people recover from it, supporting the chapter's section on seeing rejection as fit.

4. Who Gets In and Why: A Year Inside College Admissions - Jeffrey Selingo - Scribner (2020) - Selingo embedded with admissions offices at Emory, Davidson and the University of Washington. His framing of colleges as "buyers" and "sellers" explains how institutional priorities, yield and waitlists shape who is admitted.

5. The Truth about College Admission: A Family Guide to Getting In and Staying Together - Brennan Barnard and Rick Clark - Johns Hopkins University Press (2019) - Written by a high school counselor and Georgia Tech's director of undergraduate admission, it explains each step from both sides of the desk, stressing that decisions reflect a college's institutional priorities, not a verdict on the student.

6. [What to Do If You're Waitlisted](https://bigfuture.collegeboard.org/plan-for-college/apply-to-college/after-youve-applied/what-to-do-if-youre-waitlisted) - College Board BigFuture - Steps to take after a waitlist decision, from confirming your spot to sending updates, matching the chapter's waitlist strategy. Follow it soon after your decision arrives.

7. [You Got Accepted. Now What?](https://bigfuture.collegeboard.org/plan-for-college/apply-to-college/after-youve-applied/you-got-accepted-now-what) - College Board BigFuture - What to do after an acceptance, from reviewing aid to responding by the deadline. Use it alongside the chapter's Decision Response Guide MicroSim.

8. [No Acceptance Letters? You Still Have Options](https://bigfuture.collegeboard.org/plan-for-college/apply-to-college/after-youve-applied/no-acceptance-letters-you-still-have-options) - College Board BigFuture - Options for students who receive no acceptances, including late applications, community college and gap years. A reassuring read if spring decisions don't go as hoped.

9. [What to Do When Your Financial Aid Award Isn't Enough](https://bigfuture.collegeboard.org/pay-for-college/get-help-paying-for-college/scholarships-grants-institutional-aid/what-to-do-when-your-financial-aid-award-isnt-enough) - College Board BigFuture - How to appeal an aid offer with new information, supporting the chapter's financial aid appeals section. It explains what documents to send and how to phrase the request.

10. [College Acceptance Letters](https://bigfuture.collegeboard.org/plan-for-college/college-acceptance-letters) - College Board BigFuture - Explains what acceptance letters include and how to read conditions, deadlines and next steps. Read every condition in your letter so you don't risk a rescinded offer.
