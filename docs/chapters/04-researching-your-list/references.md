# References: Researching and Narrowing Your College List

1. [College Scorecard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/College_Scorecard) - Wikipedia - History and contents of the U.S. Department of Education's college data tool, including cost, graduation rates and earnings, one of the chapter's main research tools.

2. [Common Data Set](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Common_Data_Set) - Wikipedia - Explains the standardized report colleges publish each year with admission, test score and aid data, and how it allows fair comparisons between schools.

3. [College tour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/College_tour) - Wikipedia - Describes campus visits and tours, what students typically see and how visits fit into college research, matching the chapter's experience tools.

4. Fiske Guide to Colleges (annual) - Edward B. Fiske - Sourcebooks - Fiske, a former New York Times education editor, built his guide on candid student surveys rather than statistics alone. His profiles of academic and social life model the kind of research this chapter recommends.

5. The College Solution (2nd ed.) - Lynn O'Shaughnessy - FT Press (2012) - O'Shaughnessy is known for teaching families to research colleges by net price, graduation rates and outcomes instead of rankings, the same evidence-first approach this chapter uses to narrow a list.

6. [College Scorecard](https://collegescorecard.ed.gov/) - U.S. Department of Education - Official search tool for comparing colleges on average cost, graduation rate, debt and earnings. Use it alongside the chapter's College Outcomes Comparison MicroSim.

7. [College Navigator](https://nces.ed.gov/collegenavigator/) - National Center for Education Statistics - Federal database with detailed admissions, enrollment, retention and graduation data for every accredited college, useful for checking the numbers this chapter discusses.

8. [10 Ways to Learn About Colleges Online](https://bigfuture.collegeboard.org/plan-for-college/find-your-fit/10-ways-to-learn-about-colleges-online) - College Board BigFuture - Practical list of online research methods, from virtual tours to student blogs and information sessions, for students who can't visit every campus.

9. [6 Steps to Get the Most Out of a Campus Visit](https://bigfuture.collegeboard.org/plan-for-college/find-your-fit/6-steps-to-get-the-most-out-of-a-campus-visit) - College Board BigFuture - Checklist for planning and making the most of a campus visit, including questions to ask and things to notice, supporting the chapter's campus visit section.

10. [College Fair Checklist](https://bigfuture.collegeboard.org/plan-for-college/find-your-fit/college-fair-checklist) - College Board BigFuture - Step-by-step preparation for college fairs, including questions to ask representatives and how to follow up, matching the chapter's college fairs section.
