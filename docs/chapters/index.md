# Chapters

This textbook is organized into 20 chapters covering 342 concepts.

## Chapter Overview

1. [The College Landscape and Application Rounds](01-college-landscape-and-rounds/index.md) - An introduction to the admissions process and timeline, the kinds of four-year colleges, residency and selectivity, and how Early Decision, Early Action, Regular Decision, and rolling admission work.
2. [How Colleges Evaluate Applicants](02-how-colleges-evaluate/index.md) - How admissions offices read applications, from holistic and formula-based review to transcripts, GPA, rigor, class rank, and institutional priorities, plus the myths that distort how students see the process.
3. [Finding Your Fit](03-finding-your-fit/index.md) - A self-assessment of priorities, major, size, setting, culture, and cost that turns into academic, social, and financial fit and a list balanced across reach, target, and safety schools.
4. [Researching and Narrowing Your College List](04-researching-your-list/index.md) - Using the College Scorecard, Common Data Set, outcome data, visits, fairs, and reviews to research schools, then narrowing a long list to a final eight to twelve.
5. [Getting Organized](05-getting-organized/index.md) - Setting up the systems that carry you through senior year: deadline trackers, filing systems, a dedicated email, portals and the Common App account, counselors, family roles, and five first steps.
6. [The SAT and ACT Question](06-sat-act-question/index.md) - Deciding whether and where to submit SAT or ACT scores under test-optional, test-required, and test-blind policies, with superscoring, retesting, registration, and free, effective prep.
7. [Your Senior Year Course Load](07-senior-year-course-load/index.md) - Why senior grades, the mid-year report, and the final transcript still count, and how to choose AP, IB, or dual enrollment while balancing rigor against burnout.
8. [Activities and Resume](08-activities-and-resume/index.md) - Presenting extracurriculars, jobs, and family responsibilities as strengths, showing depth and impact, and turning them into a strong activities list and resume.
9. [The Personal Essay](09-personal-essay/index.md) - Writing the Common App personal essay from brainstorming and topic choice through structure, authentic voice, reflection, drafting, feedback, and revision.
10. [Supplemental Essays](10-supplemental-essays/index.md) - Researching each school to write specific Why Us, major, community, and activity essays and short answers, and adapting material without falling into the recycled-essay trap.
11. [Letters of Recommendation](11-letters-of-recommendation/index.md) - Choosing and asking teacher and counselor recommenders, preparing a brag sheet and recommender packet, the FERPA waiver, and following up with thanks.
12. [Filling Out the Common App and Coalition App](12-common-app-coalition-app/index.md) - A section-by-section walkthrough of the Common App and Coalition App, from profile to supplements, plus fees and waivers, submission, and sending transcripts and scores.
13. [Interviews](13-interviews/index.md) - The kinds of college interviews, how to prepare and practice, what questions to ask, and how to follow up.
14. [Understanding Financial Aid](14-understanding-financial-aid/index.md) - How college pricing and aid work, from net price and the FAFSA and CSS Profile to demonstrated need, types of aid, need-blind admissions, grants, verification, and priority deadlines.
15. [Scholarships](15-scholarships/index.md) - Finding automatic, competitive, outside, and local scholarships, deciding which are worth applying for, reusing essays, keeping renewable awards, and avoiding scams and award displacement.
16. [Comparing Award Letters](16-comparing-award-letters/index.md) - Reading and comparing award letters line by line, separating grants from loans and work-study, projecting four-year cost and debt, and requesting a reconsideration.
17. [Waiting and Managing Anxiety](17-waiting-and-anxiety/index.md) - What happens to an application after submission and healthy ways to get through the wait, from portal checking to limiting comparison, self-care, and support.
18. [Handling Decisions](18-handling-decisions/index.md) - Responding to acceptances, denials, deferrals, and waitlists, handling Early Decision release and rescinded admission, appealing aid, and seeing rejection as a question of fit.
19. [Making Your Final Choice](19-making-your-final-choice/index.md) - Comparing offers side by side, using admitted-student days and revisits, committing by May 1 with an enrollment deposit, and declining other offers the right way.
20. [Getting Ready for Fall](20-getting-ready-for-fall/index.md) - Every step from deposit to move-in, including housing, orientation, health forms, AP credit, placement, registration, and summer melt, closing with the senior-year planner and capstone roadmap.

## How to Use This Textbook

The chapters follow the order of senior year: understanding how admissions works, building your list, testing, writing and submitting applications, paying for college, and making your decision. Each chapter only builds on concepts introduced in earlier chapters, so reading in order gives the smoothest path. Because every chapter lists its prerequisites, you can also jump straight to the topic that is most urgent for you right now and look back only where needed.
