# References: The SAT and ACT Question

1. [SAT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SAT) - Wikipedia - Comprehensive history and current format of the SAT, including the digital adaptive test, section scores and score ranges, background for the chapter's SAT section.

2. [ACT (test)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ACT_(test)) - Wikipedia - Describes the ACT's sections, scoring, composite score and format changes, helping students compare it with the SAT as the chapter suggests.

3. [PSAT/NMSQT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PSAT/NMSQT) - Wikipedia - Explains the PSAT/NMSQT, its link to the National Merit Scholarship Program and how it serves as practice for the SAT.

4. The Official Digital SAT Study Guide - The College Board - College Board - The test maker's own guide with full-length practice tests and answer explanations. Official practice material is the benchmark the chapter recommends over expensive commercial prep.

5. The Official ACT Prep Guide (annual) - ACT, Inc. - Wiley - Contains real, previously administered ACT tests with explanations. Using the test maker's own questions is the most reliable way to run the diagnostic test and timed practice the chapter describes.

6. [SAT Practice Tests](https://satsuite.collegeboard.org/practice/practice-tests) - College Board - Free full-length digital SAT practice tests in the Bluebook app, with score reports. The best source for the diagnostic and timed practice tests in this chapter.

7. [Digital SAT Prep](https://www.khanacademy.org/test-prep/digital-sat) - Khan Academy - Free, official SAT practice built with the College Board, with lessons and questions targeted to each skill. Supports the chapter's free test prep and error log strategies.

8. [ACT Test Preparation](https://www.act.org/content/act/en/products-and-services/the-act/test-preparation.html) - ACT, Inc. - The test maker's free and paid preparation resources, including practice tests, for students choosing the ACT. It also links to the official ACT practice test and score guides.

9. [What Is Test Optional?](https://bigfuture.collegeboard.org/plan-for-college/apply-to-college/what-is-test-optional) - College Board BigFuture - Explains test-optional, test-required and test-blind policies and how to decide whether to submit scores, matching the chapter's testing policy section.

10. [Should I Retake the SAT?](https://bigfuture.collegeboard.org/plan-for-college/apply-to-college/should-i-retake-sat) - College Board BigFuture - Guidance on when a retest is likely to help, how superscoring works and how to plan a second test date, supporting the chapter's retesting decision.
