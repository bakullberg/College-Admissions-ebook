# References: Your Senior Year Course Load

1. [Advanced Placement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Advanced_Placement) - Wikipedia - History and structure of the AP program, its exams and 1–5 scores, and how colleges award credit, background for the chapter's AP section.

2. [International Baccalaureate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Baccalaureate) - Wikipedia - Describes the IB Diploma Programme, its courses and assessments, and how it compares with AP, matching the chapter's IB section.

3. [Dual enrollment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dual_enrollment) - Wikipedia - Explains how high school students take college courses for credit, including benefits and transfer limits, supporting the chapter's dual enrollment section.

4. Doing School: How We Are Creating a Generation of Stressed-Out, Materialistic, and Miseducated Students - Denise Clark Pope - Yale University Press (2001) - Pope followed five high-achieving students and named the habit of chasing grades over learning "doing school." Her work underlies the chapter's advice on balancing rigor against burnout.

5. Overloaded and Underprepared: Strategies for Stronger Schools and Healthy, Successful Kids - Denise Pope, Maureen Brown and Sarah Miles - Jossey-Bass (2015) - Drawing on Stanford's Challenge Success research, the authors show how heavy course loads affect health and learning, and offer practical strategies for a sustainable schedule.

6. [About AP Scores](https://apstudents.collegeboard.org/about-ap-scores) - College Board AP Students - Explains the 1–5 AP score scale, when scores are released and how they are sent to colleges, matching the chapter's AP score section.

7. [AP Credit Policy Search](https://apstudents.collegeboard.org/getting-credit-placement/search-policies) - College Board AP Students - Searchable database of each college's AP credit and placement policies, useful for checking how your exams will count. Look up each college on your list.

8. [National Alliance of Concurrent Enrollment Partnerships](https://www.nacep.org/) - NACEP - The national organization for dual and concurrent enrollment, with research and guidance on taking college courses in high school. It includes questions to ask about transferring credits.

9. [How and Why to Take Advanced High School Classes](https://bigfuture.collegeboard.org/plan-for-college/stand-out-in-high-school/how-and-why-to-take-advanced-high-school-classes) - College Board BigFuture - Explains the benefits of honors, AP and other advanced courses and how to choose a challenging but manageable schedule. It also covers when to talk with your counselor.

10. [Challenge Success](https://challengesuccess.org/) - Challenge Success, Stanford Graduate School of Education - Research-based resources for students and families on academic stress, workload and well-being, supporting the chapter's section on balancing rigor and burnout.
