# References: Interviews

1. [Interview](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interview) - Wikipedia - General overview of interviews as structured conversations, their purposes and formats, background for the kinds of college interviews this chapter describes.

2. [Mock interview](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mock_interview) - Wikipedia - Explains practice interviews and why rehearsing answers aloud improves performance, supporting the chapter's practice interview section. It covers what to say, how long to practice and how to ask for feedback.

3. [Videotelephony](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Videotelephony) - Wikipedia - Covers video calling technology and etiquette considerations, relevant to the chapter's section on virtual interviews. Test your camera, sound and lighting before a virtual interview.

4. College Admission: From Application to Acceptance, Step by Step - Robin Mamlet and Christine VanDeVelde - Three Rivers Press (2011) - Mamlet, former dean of admission at Stanford, Swarthmore and Sarah Lawrence, built this step-by-step guide on interviews with deans and counselors, organizing the whole process around a senior-year calendar of tasks.

5. What Color Is Your Parachute? - Richard N. Bolles - Ten Speed Press - Bolles reframed interviews as a two-way conversation in which both sides are deciding, not an interrogation. That framing matches this chapter's advice to treat a college interview as a conversation and prepare real questions.

6. [College Interviews: The Basics](https://bigfuture.collegeboard.org/plan-for-college/apply-to-college/application-process/college-interviews-the-basics) - College Board BigFuture - Explains the types of college interviews, what interviewers look for and how to prepare. It also explains how much interviews count at different colleges.

7. [College Interviews: Practice Questions and Strategies](https://bigfuture.collegeboard.org/plan-for-college/apply-to-college/application-process/college-interviews-practice-questions-and-strategies) - College Board BigFuture - Common interview questions with strategies for answering them, a companion to the chapter's Interview Practice Deck MicroSim. Practice them aloud with a friend or counselor.

8. [College Interview Tips](https://www.princetonreview.com/college-advice/college-interview-tips) - The Princeton Review - Practical tips on preparing, dressing, asking questions and following up after a college interview. Most of the advice applies to alumni interviews as well.

9. [Campus Visits and Tours](https://bigfuture.collegeboard.org/plan-for-college/find-your-fit/campus-visits-and-tours) - College Board BigFuture - Guide to campus visits, where on-campus interviews are often scheduled, with advice on making the most of time with admissions staff.

10. [Common App Applicant Help Center](https://appsupport.commonapp.org/applicantsupport/s/) - Common App - Help articles for finding each college's interview options and requirements within the Common App. Check each college's section for interview deadlines.
