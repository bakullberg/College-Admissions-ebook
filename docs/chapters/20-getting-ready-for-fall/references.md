# References: Getting Ready for Fall

1. [Summer melt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Summer_melt) - Wikipedia - Explains the phenomenon of admitted students not enrolling in the fall and the research on preventing it, the central idea of this chapter's health and finances section.

2. [Student orientation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Student_orientation) - Wikipedia - Describes college orientation programs and what new students do during them, matching the chapter's orientation section. It covers registration, advising and meeting other new students.

3. [Placement testing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Placement_testing) - Wikipedia - Explains how colleges use placement tests to assign students to the right courses, supporting the chapter's placement tests section. Ask whether your AP scores exempt you.

4. Summer Melt: Supporting Low-Income Students Through the Transition to College - Benjamin L. Castleman and Lindsay C. Page - Harvard Education Press (2014) - Castleman and Page documented summer melt and showed that simple supports, such as text-message reminders and counselor check-ins, help students complete summer tasks and enroll.

5. The Privileged Poor: How Elite Colleges Are Failing Disadvantaged Students - Anthony Abraham Jack - Harvard University Press (2019) - Jack distinguishes the "privileged poor" from the "doubly disadvantaged," showing why some first-generation students struggle to navigate campus resources and why knowing how to ask for help matters.

6. [Off to College Checklist](https://bigfuture.collegeboard.org/plan-for-college/apply-to-college/after-youve-applied/off-to-college-checklist) - College Board BigFuture - Checklist of summer tasks from housing and health forms to orientation, a companion to the chapter's Summer Countdown MicroSim. Print it and check items off.

7. [What to Do the Summer Before College](https://bigfuture.collegeboard.org/pay-for-college/get-help-paying-for-college/what-to-do-summer-before-college) - College Board BigFuture - Financial steps to complete before fall, including reviewing bills, accepting aid and setting a budget. Missing one of these steps is a common cause of summer melt.

8. [College Roommates: The Basics](https://bigfuture.collegeboard.org/plan-for-college/get-started/college-roommates-the-basics) - College Board BigFuture - Advice on roommate selection and living together, matching the chapter's roommate selection section. It also offers tips for working through early disagreements with a roommate.

9. [What Are College Placement Tests?](https://bigfuture.collegeboard.org/plan-for-college/apply-to-college/after-youve-applied/what-are-college-placement-tests) - College Board BigFuture - Explains why colleges give placement tests and how to prepare. Taking them seriously can save you from repeating courses you've already mastered.

10. [University of Iowa Admissions](https://admissions.uiowa.edu/) - University of Iowa - Official admissions site for the University of Iowa, the school used in the chapter's capstone application roadmap. Use it to check current deadlines and requirements for your own roadmap.
