# References: Activities and Resume

1. [Extracurricular activity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Extracurricular_activity) - Wikipedia - Overview of activities outside the classroom, from sports and arts to clubs, and their role in student development and college admission.

2. [Volunteering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volunteering) - Wikipedia - Describes volunteer and community service work and its benefits, background for the chapter's community service section. It also explains why sustained service matters more than one-time hours.

3. [Résumé](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/R%C3%A9sum%C3%A9) - Wikipedia - Explains the purpose and common formats of a résumé, useful context for the activities resume the chapter shows students how to build.

4. Grit: The Power of Passion and Perseverance - Angela Duckworth - Scribner (2016) - Duckworth describes research showing that years of follow-through in a high school activity predicted later achievement, the evidence behind this chapter's emphasis on sustained commitment and depth over breadth.

5. How to Be a High School Superstar - Cal Newport - Broadway Books (2010) - Newport argues that one deep, unusual interest impresses more than a long list, and that students should underschedule to make room for it. A memorable case against the well-rounded applicant myth.

6. [Extracurriculars Matter to You and to Colleges](https://bigfuture.collegeboard.org/plan-for-college/stand-out-in-high-school/extracurriculars-matter-to-you-and-to-colleges) - College Board BigFuture - Explains what colleges learn from activities and why commitment and leadership matter more than the number of clubs. Read it before you write your activities list.

7. [High School Clubs and Extracurricular Activities](https://bigfuture.collegeboard.org/plan-for-college/stand-out-in-high-school/high-school-clubs-extracurricular-activities) - College Board BigFuture - Survey of activity types, including jobs and family responsibilities, supporting the chapter's section on kinds of activities worth listing. It may help you remember activities you'd overlooked.

8. [What's a High School Resume?](https://bigfuture.collegeboard.org/plan-for-college/stand-out-in-high-school/whats-high-school-resume) - College Board BigFuture - Guide to building a high school resume, what to include and how to format it, a companion to the chapter's activities resume section.

9. [Balancing High School and Part-Time Work](https://bigfuture.collegeboard.org/plan-for-college/stand-out-in-high-school/stay-motivated/balancing-high-school-and-part-time-work) - College Board BigFuture - Advice on managing a job alongside school and why part-time work counts as a meaningful activity on applications. It also covers how to describe a job's responsibilities.

10. [The Importance of Volunteering in High School](https://bigfuture.collegeboard.org/plan-for-college/stand-out-in-high-school/importance-of-volunteering-in-high-school) - College Board BigFuture - Explains how community service builds skills and how to describe it effectively on an application. It includes ideas for finding service that connects to your interests.
