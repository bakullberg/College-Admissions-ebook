# References: The Personal Essay

1. [Application essay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Application_essay) - Wikipedia - Overview of admission essays and personal statements, their purpose and common prompts, background for this chapter's guide to the Common App essay.

2. [Show, don't tell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Show,_don%27t_tell) - Wikipedia - Explains the writing technique of revealing ideas through scenes, actions and detail rather than summary, the core skill in the chapter's writing techniques section.

3. [Story structure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Story_structure) - Wikipedia - Describes how narratives are organized, from openings to turning points and resolution, useful background for the essay structures the chapter teaches.

4. College Essay Essentials: A Step-by-Step Guide to Writing a Successful College Admission Essay - Ethan Sawyer - Sourcebooks (2016) - Sawyer, known as the College Essay Guy, popularized the distinction between narrative and montage essay structures and the "Feelings and Needs" brainstorming exercise. Both shape this chapter's approach.

5. On Writing the College Application Essay (25th anniversary ed.) - Harry Bauld - Harper Perennial (2012) - A former admissions reader and English teacher, Bauld is known for his blunt catalog of clichéd topics and his case for small, specific stories told in an authentic voice.

6. [The Ultimate College Essay Guide](https://bigfuture.collegeboard.org/plan-for-college/apply-to-college/application-process/ultimate-college-essay-guide) - College Board BigFuture - Step-by-step guide from brainstorming to final draft, with advice on choosing a topic and finding your voice. It also covers how to respond to the Common App prompts.

7. [Tips for Writing an Effective Application Essay](https://bigfuture.collegeboard.org/plan-for-college/apply-to-college/application-process/tips-for-writing-an-effective-application-essay) - College Board BigFuture - Practical tips on topic choice, specific detail and revision, reinforcing the chapter's writing techniques. It's a quick checklist to use before sharing a draft.

8. [6 Tips for Crafting Your Best College Essay](https://bigfuture.collegeboard.org/plan-for-college/apply-to-college/application-process/6-tips-for-crafting-your-best-college-essay) - College Board BigFuture - Short, concrete advice on being specific, staying authentic and getting feedback, with links to sample essays. Read the samples to see the techniques in action.

9. [The Writing Process](https://owl.purdue.edu/owl/general_writing/the_writing_process/index.html) - Purdue Online Writing Lab - Guides to prewriting, drafting and revising from Purdue's writing lab, supporting the chapter's section on moving from draft to final essay.

10. [Revising Drafts](https://writingcenter.unc.edu/tips-and-tools/revising-drafts/) - UNC Writing Center - Explains how to revise for ideas and structure before editing sentences, with strategies such as reverse outlining that apply well to personal essays.
