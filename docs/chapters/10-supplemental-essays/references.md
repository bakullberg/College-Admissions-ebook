# References: Supplemental Essays

1. [Application essay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Application_essay) - Wikipedia - Covers the range of admission essays, including college-specific prompts, background for the supplemental essay types this chapter describes. Several prompt types are summarized there.

2. [Yield (college admissions)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yield_(college_admissions)) - Wikipedia - Explains yield, the share of admitted students who enroll, and why colleges use "Why us?" essays and demonstrated interest to predict it.

3. [Academic major](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_major) - Wikipedia - Describes how majors work at U.S. colleges, useful context for writing a "Why this major?" essay. Knowing the major's courses helps you write specifically.

4. Fiske Real College Essays That Work (2nd ed.) - Edward B. Fiske and Bruce G. Hammond - Sourcebooks - A collection of real admission essays, including college-specific supplements, with commentary on why each works. Useful models for the specificity this chapter asks for.

5. 50 Successful Harvard Application Essays - Staff of The Harvard Crimson - St. Martin's Griffin - Real essays from admitted students, each followed by a short critique from Crimson editors. The critiques show how readers respond to specificity, voice and fit.

6. [What's the Common App?](https://bigfuture.collegeboard.org/plan-for-college/apply-to-college/whats-the-common-app) - College Board BigFuture - Explains how the Common App's shared sections and each college's own questions fit together, including where supplemental essays appear. Check each college's section early.

7. [College Applications: How to Begin](https://bigfuture.collegeboard.org/plan-for-college/apply-to-college/college-applications-how-to-begin) - College Board BigFuture - Advice on planning applications, including gathering each college's requirements, a first step in the supplement planning the chapter describes. Start with a list of every prompt.

8. [Why Visit Colleges?](https://bigfuture.collegeboard.org/plan-for-college/find-your-fit/why-visit-colleges) - College Board BigFuture - Explains how visits and information sessions reveal what makes a college distinctive, the school-specific research a strong "Why us?" essay needs.

9. [Tips and Tools](https://writingcenter.unc.edu/tips-and-tools/) - UNC Writing Center - Handouts on concision, word choice and revision, helpful for fitting a specific answer into a short supplemental word limit. Try its concision handout first.

10. [Purdue Online Writing Lab](https://owl.purdue.edu/) - Purdue University - Free writing guides covering clarity, conciseness and audience, useful when adapting one essay idea to several colleges' prompts. It's free and searchable by topic.
