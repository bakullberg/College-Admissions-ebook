# References: Scholarships

1. [Scholarship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scholarship) - Wikipedia - Overview of scholarship types, including merit, need-based and outside awards, background for the chapter's sections on finding and keeping scholarships.

2. [National Merit Scholarship Program](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Merit_Scholarship_Program) - Wikipedia - Describes the competitive scholarship program tied to the PSAT/NMSQT, an example of the competitive scholarships this chapter discusses. It also explains semifinalist and finalist stages.

3. [Advance-fee scam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Advance-fee_scam) - Wikipedia - Explains scams that ask for upfront payment in exchange for promised money, the pattern behind most scholarship scams the chapter warns about.

4. The Ultimate Scholarship Book (annual) - Gen Tanabe and Kelly Tanabe - SuperCollege - The Tanabes, who paid for Harvard with outside scholarships, are known for practical strategies on local awards and reusing essays, matching this chapter's advice on prioritizing scholarships.

5. Debt-Free U: How I Paid for an Outstanding College Education Without Loans, Scholarships, or Mooching off My Parents - Zac Bissonnette - Portfolio (2010) - Bissonnette argues that choosing an affordable college saves far more than chasing small outside awards, a useful counterweight when weighing the expected value of applying.

6. [Finding and Applying for Scholarships](https://studentaid.gov/understand-aid/types/scholarships) - Federal Student Aid - Official guidance on where to find legitimate scholarships and how they affect other aid. It also explains how scholarships can change your other aid.

7. [Avoiding Student Aid Scams](https://studentaid.gov/resources/scams) - Federal Student Aid - Federal guide to recognizing and reporting scholarship and financial aid scams, matching the chapter's scam warning signs. It also explains how to report a suspected scam.

8. [BigFuture Scholarship Search](https://bigfuture.collegeboard.org/scholarship-search) - College Board - Free scholarship search engine with filters for eligibility, a starting point for building a scholarship list. Save matching awards to a list and track their deadlines.

9. [How Outside Scholarships Affect Your Financial Aid](https://bigfuture.collegeboard.org/pay-for-college/get-help-paying-for-college/scholarships-grants-institutional-aid/how-outside-scholarships-affect-your-financial-aid) - College Board BigFuture - Explains how colleges may reduce other aid when you win outside awards, the award displacement covered in this chapter. Read it before accepting any outside award.

10. [How to Avoid Financial Aid Scams](https://bigfuture.collegeboard.org/pay-for-college/get-help-paying-for-college/how-to-avoid-financial-aid-scams) - College Board BigFuture - Warning signs of fraudulent scholarship and aid offers, a companion to the chapter's Scholarship or Scam? MicroSim. Use it to test any offer that seems too good to be true.
