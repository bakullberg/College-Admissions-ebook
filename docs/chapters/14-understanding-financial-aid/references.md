# References: Understanding Financial Aid

1. [FAFSA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FAFSA) - Wikipedia - History and structure of the Free Application for Federal Student Aid, including the recent FAFSA simplification and the Student Aid Index.

2. [CSS Profile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CSS_Profile) - Wikipedia - Describes the College Board's supplemental aid application used by many private colleges to award their own grants. It also explains how the CSS Profile differs from the FAFSA.

3. [Pell Grant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pell_Grant) - Wikipedia - Explains the federal government's main need-based grant for undergraduates, its history and how eligibility is determined. Pell eligibility appears on every award letter you receive.

4. Paying for College (annual) - Kalman Chany - The Princeton Review / Penguin Random House - Chany is known for his line-by-line walkthroughs of the FAFSA and CSS Profile and for explaining how aid formulas treat income and assets, making the forms in this chapter far less intimidating.

5. The Price You Pay for College - Ron Lieber - Harper (2021) - The New York Times money columnist explains how colleges use merit aid and tuition discounting to compete for students, and reframes the family question from "what will it cost?" to "what is it worth to us?"

6. [FAFSA Application](https://studentaid.gov/h/apply-for-aid/fafsa) - Federal Student Aid - The official, free FAFSA site, with the application, deadlines and step-by-step help for students and parents. Every student should file it, even families who expect little need-based aid.

7. [What Is the Student Aid Index (SAI)?](https://studentaid.gov/help-center/answers/article/what-is-sai) - Federal Student Aid - Official explanation of how the SAI is calculated and used by aid offices, matching the chapter's Student Aid Index section.

8. [Listing Colleges on the FAFSA Form](https://studentaid.gov/apply-for-aid/fafsa/filling-out/school-list) - Federal Student Aid - How to add colleges and federal school codes to the FAFSA, supporting the chapter's federal school code section. Check each college's deadline too.

9. [CSS Profile](https://cssprofile.collegeboard.org/) - College Board - The official CSS Profile site, with the application, fee waiver information and the list of colleges that require it. Run one for every college on your list.

10. [Net Price Calculator Center](https://collegecost.ed.gov/net-price) - U.S. Department of Education - Links to every college's net price calculator, the tool the chapter recommends for estimating what your family will actually pay.
