# References: Waiting and Managing Anxiety

1. [Anxiety](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anxiety) - Wikipedia - Explains anxiety as an emotion and how it differs from anxiety disorders, background for the chapter's section on managing uncertainty.

2. [Coping](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coping) - Wikipedia - Describes coping strategies and the difference between healthy and unhealthy ways of handling stress, supporting the chapter's healthy coping strategies section.

3. [Social comparison theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Social_comparison_theory) - Wikipedia - Explains why people judge themselves against others and how comparison affects mood, the research behind the chapter's advice on limiting comparison.

4. Under Pressure: Confronting the Epidemic of Stress and Anxiety in Girls - Lisa Damour - Ballantine Books (2019) - Clinical psychologist Damour is known for reframing stress and anxiety as normal and even useful in manageable amounts. Her practical strategies apply to any student waiting on decisions.

5. The Gift of Failure: How the Best Parents Learn to Let Go So Their Children Can Succeed - Jessica Lahey - Harper (2015) - Lahey, a teacher, argues that setbacks build competence and resilience. Her perspective helps students and parents keep disappointing news in proportion.

6. [Anxiety Disorders](https://www.nimh.nih.gov/health/topics/anxiety-disorders) - National Institute of Mental Health - Explains the signs of anxiety disorders and when to seek help, useful for telling normal worry from something that needs support.

7. [Manage Stress: Strengthen Your Support Network](https://www.apa.org/topics/stress/manage-social-support) - American Psychological Association - Explains how friends, family and mentors buffer stress and how to build a support network, matching the chapter's support network section.

8. [The Jed Foundation](https://jedfoundation.org/) - The Jed Foundation - Nonprofit resources on emotional health for teens and young adults, including coping with stress and transitions. Its guides on handling uncertainty and big transitions fit the waiting period well.

9. [988 Suicide and Crisis Lifeline](https://988lifeline.org/) - 988 Lifeline - Free, confidential support by call, text or chat, 24 hours a day, for anyone in emotional distress. Save it in your phone so you can call or text 988 any time you need it.

10. [Resilience Guide for Parents and Teachers](https://www.apa.org/topics/resilience/guide-parents-teachers) - American Psychological Association - Practical tips for building resilience in young people, including routines, self-care and keeping perspective. Parents can use it to support a student through the waiting period.
