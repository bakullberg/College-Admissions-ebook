# References: How Colleges Evaluate Applicants

1. [Grading in education](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grading_in_education) - Wikipedia - Covers grading scales and grade point averages, including weighted and unweighted systems. Background for the chapter's explanation of how colleges read and recalculate GPA.

2. [Class rank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Class_rank) - Wikipedia - Explains how schools rank students, why many have stopped reporting rank and how colleges use it when it is available, matching the chapter's class rank section.

3. [Legacy preferences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legacy_preferences) - Wikipedia - Describes admission preferences for children of alumni, their history and recent changes, as one example of the institutional priorities this chapter discusses.

4. Who Gets In and Why: A Year Inside College Admissions - Jeffrey Selingo - Scribner (2020) - Selingo embedded with admissions offices at Emory, Davidson and the University of Washington. His framing of colleges as "buyers" and "sellers" explains how institutional priorities, yield and waitlists shape who is admitted.

5. Admissions Confidential: An Insider's Account of the Elite College Selection Process - Rachel Toor - St. Martin's Press (2001) - A former Duke admissions officer's candid account of reading thousands of files, showing how quickly readers judge transcripts, essays and recommendations, and what makes a letter or essay stand out from the stack.

6. [High School Classes Colleges Look For](https://bigfuture.collegeboard.org/plan-for-college/stand-out-in-high-school/high-school-classes-colleges-look-for) - College Board BigFuture - Lists the core academic courses most colleges expect and explains why course rigor matters, supporting the chapter's sections on core courses and rigor.

7. [How to Calculate GPA on a 4.0 Scale](https://bigfuture.collegeboard.org/plan-for-college/get-started/how-to-calculate-gpa-4.0-scale) - College Board BigFuture - Step-by-step GPA calculation with worked examples of weighted and unweighted grades. Pairs well with the chapter's GPA Calculator MicroSim. It includes a quick reference table.

8. [5 Things You Need to Know About College Admission](https://bigfuture.collegeboard.org/plan-for-college/apply-to-college/5-things-you-need-to-know-about-college-admission) - College Board BigFuture - Short overview of what admission officers weigh, from grades and rigor to essays and activities, reinforcing the chapter's picture of holistic review.

9. [Character Counts: What Are Colleges Looking For?](https://bigfuture.collegeboard.org/plan-for-college/stand-out-in-high-school/character-counts-what-are-colleges-looking-for) - College Board BigFuture - Explains the personal qualities colleges look for beyond grades and scores, connecting to the chapter's discussion of holistic review and institutional priorities.

10. [What's in a College Application?](https://bigfuture.collegeboard.org/plan-for-college/apply-to-college/whats-in-a-college-application) - College Board BigFuture - Lists each component of a typical application and what it tells a reader, useful for seeing how the transcript, scores and essays fit together.
