# References: Filling Out the Common App and Coalition App

1. [Common App](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Common_App) - Wikipedia - History and structure of the Common Application, including its shared sections, member colleges and writing supplements. Background for the chapter's walkthrough of each section.

2. [Coalition for College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coalition_for_College) - Wikipedia - Describes the Coalition for College and its application platform, an alternative to the Common App used by some colleges. Check which platforms your colleges accept.

3. [College Board](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/College_Board) - Wikipedia - Explains the organization that runs the SAT, AP and CSS Profile, and its role in sending official score reports to colleges.

4. College Admission: From Application to Acceptance, Step by Step - Robin Mamlet and Christine VanDeVelde - Three Rivers Press (2011) - Mamlet, former dean of admission at Stanford, Swarthmore and Sarah Lawrence, built this step-by-step guide on interviews with deans and counselors, organizing the whole process around a senior-year calendar of tasks.

5. The Truth about College Admission: A Family Guide to Getting In and Staying Together - Brennan Barnard and Rick Clark - Johns Hopkins University Press (2019) - Written by a high school counselor and Georgia Tech's director of undergraduate admission, it explains each step from both sides of the desk, stressing that decisions reflect a college's institutional priorities, not a verdict on the student.

6. [Common App Applicant Help Center](https://appsupport.commonapp.org/applicantsupport/s/) - Common App - Official help articles for every section of the Common App, from the profile and education sections to previewing and submitting.

7. [What's the Common App?](https://bigfuture.collegeboard.org/plan-for-college/apply-to-college/whats-the-common-app) - College Board BigFuture - Overview of how the Common App works, which colleges accept it and what each section asks for. Useful when deciding which platform to use for each college on your list.

8. [College Application Fee Waiver FAQ](https://bigfuture.collegeboard.org/plan-for-college/apply-to-college/college-application-fee-waiver-faq) - College Board BigFuture - Answers common questions about who qualifies for application fee waivers and how to request them, supporting the chapter's fees and waivers section.

9. [What Is the SRAR?](https://bigfuture.collegeboard.org/plan-for-college/apply-to-college/what-is-srar) - College Board BigFuture - Explains the Self-Reported Academic Record some colleges require, related to the chapter's self-reported academic records section. Check whether each of your colleges requires one before you start.

10. [What You Need to Know About Sending Your SAT Scores](https://bigfuture.collegeboard.org/plan-for-college/stand-out-in-high-school/what-you-need-know-about-sending-your-sat-scores) - College Board BigFuture - Explains how and when to send official SAT scores, including institution codes, supporting the chapter's section on sending official documents.
